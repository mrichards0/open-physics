With upward positive and no air resistance, what is a rock's acceleration during a vertical toss?

It is -g on the way up, at the top, and on the way down.It is -g up, zero at the top, and +g down.It is +g during the entire flight.It is zero whenever the rock moves at constant speed.

Gravity remains downward throughout the flight; only velocity becomes zero at the top.
