Which sets of three known quantities leave two unknowns that require two kinematic equations?

Scenario 1:

Known acceleration, time, and displacement; solve for initial and final velocity.Known all five kinematic variables; solve for none.Known only time; solve uniquely for all others.

Scenario 2:

Known final velocity, time, and displacement; solve for initial velocity and acceleration.Known initial and final position only; solve uniquely for time and acceleration.Known acceleration only; solve uniquely for both velocities.

Each correct scenario supplies three independent knowns among the five kinematic variables and leaves a coupled pair of unknowns.
