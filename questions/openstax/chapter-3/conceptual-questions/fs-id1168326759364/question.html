Which example correctly distinguishes average speed from the magnitude of average velocity?

Complete a round trip: average speed is positive, but displacement and therefore average velocity are zero.Move straight without reversing: average speed is zero but average velocity is positive.Remain at rest: average speed differs from average velocity magnitude.Any constant-speed trip makes average velocity zero.

Average speed uses total path length; average velocity uses displacement.
