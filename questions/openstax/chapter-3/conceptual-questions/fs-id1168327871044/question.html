An astronaut has the same upward takeoff speed on the Moon and Earth. With lunar gravity one-sixth Earth's, how does maximum jump height compare?

The Moon height is 6 times the Earth height.The Moon height is 36 times the Earth height.The heights are equal.The Moon height is 1/6 the Earth height.

Maximum height is v0^2/(2g), so reducing g by six increases height by six.
