For an object thrown straight upward with no air resistance:

(a) When is velocity zero?

At the top of the trajectory.Throughout the upward trip.Just after release only.

(b) Does velocity change direction?

Yes, at the top.No, it remains upward.Only when it reaches the ground.

(c) Does acceleration have the same sign going up and down?

Yes; it remains downward both ways.No; it follows the velocity direction.Acceleration is zero on the way down.

Velocity reverses through zero at the apex, but gravitational acceleration keeps the same downward sign.
