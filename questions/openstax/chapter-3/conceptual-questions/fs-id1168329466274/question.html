An object's average velocity over a nonzero time interval is zero. What must be true?

Its displacement over the interval is zero.Its distance traveled is zero.It remained at rest for the whole interval.Its instantaneous velocity was always zero.

Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time. The object may still travel and return.
