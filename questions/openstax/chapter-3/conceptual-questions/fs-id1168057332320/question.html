Besides acceleration a(t), what initial information is needed to determine unique velocity and position functions?

An initial velocity and an initial position, each specified at a known time.Only the object mass.Only one later acceleration value.No additional information; integration constants are always zero.

Integrating acceleration introduces a velocity constant, and integrating velocity introduces a position constant. Initial conditions determine both.
