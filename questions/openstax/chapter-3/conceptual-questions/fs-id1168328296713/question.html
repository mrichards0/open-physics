For the same safe impact speed from rest, lunar gravity is one-sixth Earth's. How many times greater can the safe fall height be on the Moon?

6 times.36 times.sqrt(6) times.1/6 as high.

From v^2=2gh, fixed v gives h proportional to 1/g.
