How are instantaneous velocity and instantaneous speed related?

Speed is the nonnegative magnitude of velocity; velocity also carries direction/sign.Velocity is the magnitude of speed.They are unrelated quantities with different units.Speed is signed, while velocity is never signed.

In one dimension, speed=|v|. Direction information remains in the sign of velocity.
