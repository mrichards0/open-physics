A bacterium swims back and forth. Why can its total distance be large while its displacement is small?

Distance accumulates each leg, while oppositely directed displacements cancel.Its small size causes distance to be measured in different units.Displacement accumulates every turn, while distance cancels.A moving organism always has zero displacement.

Repeated reversals add positive path length but can return the bacterium near its starting point.
