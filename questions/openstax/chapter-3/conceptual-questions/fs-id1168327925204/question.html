A rock passes a coconut at a given height going up, then hits it at the same height coming down. Neglect air resistance. Which comparison is correct?

Its speed magnitude is the same both times; the downward hit is more likely to dislodge the coconut downward.It is faster going up because it was thrown upward.It is faster coming down because gravity adds energy on the complete trip.Its speed is zero when it returns to the coconut height.

Mechanical energy gives equal speed magnitudes at equal heights. The downward momentum direction better assists downward dislodgement.
