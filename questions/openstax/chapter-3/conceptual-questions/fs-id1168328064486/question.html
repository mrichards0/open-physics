Can velocity remain constant while acceleration is nonzero?

No. Acceleration is the time rate of change of velocity.Yes, whenever speed is constant.Yes, whenever direction is constant.Yes, if position changes linearly.

A truly constant velocity has neither changing magnitude nor changing direction, so its derivative is zero.
