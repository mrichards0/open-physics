Which situation has zero instantaneous velocity but nonzero acceleration?

A ball at the highest point of a vertical toss; v=0 momentarily while acceleration is downward.A parked car remaining at rest.A puck moving at constant velocity.An object after it has come permanently to rest on a table.

At the apex, velocity changes from upward to downward under gravity, which remains nonzero.
