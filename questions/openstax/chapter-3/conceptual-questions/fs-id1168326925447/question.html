For constant-acceleration one-dimensional motion, how many known kinematic variables are generally required to determine the remaining unknowns?

Three.One.Two.All five, leaving nothing to solve.

The five variables are displacement, initial velocity, final velocity, acceleration, and time. Three independent knowns generally allow the equations to determine the other two.
