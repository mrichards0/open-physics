Which trip clearly distinguishes distance, signed displacement, and displacement magnitude?

Walk 3 km east, then 5 km west: distance 8 km, displacement -2 km (east positive), magnitude 2 km.Walk 3 km east: all three quantities are 3 km.Remain at home: all three quantities are zero.Walk 3 km east and call both distance and displacement +8 km.

Distance accumulates every path segment. Displacement is the signed change in position, and its magnitude removes only the direction sign.
