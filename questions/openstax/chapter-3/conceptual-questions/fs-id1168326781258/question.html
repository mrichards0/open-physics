When does distance traveled equal the magnitude of displacement?

When motion follows a straight line without reversing direction.Whenever the object returns to its starting point.Whenever speed is constant, even around a curve.Only when the object does not move.

Any reversal or curved detour makes path length exceed the straight start-to-finish separation.
