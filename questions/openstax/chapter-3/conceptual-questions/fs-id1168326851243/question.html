Which device and changing feature provide a valid way to measure elapsed time?

A quartz clock: count periodic oscillations of its crystal.A ruler: count its fixed centimeter marks.A balance: observe a mass that remains constant.A compass: observe north without tracking any repeating change.

A clock uses a reliably repeating physical change; counting cycles establishes elapsed time.
