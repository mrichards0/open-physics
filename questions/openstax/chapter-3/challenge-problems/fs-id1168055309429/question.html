In a {{params.d}} m race, the winner finishes in {{params.tw}} s and second place in {{params.tl}} s. Assume each runner's speed is constant. How far behind is second place when the winner crosses?

Second place's speed is d/t_loser, so by winner time that runner covers d t_winner/t_loser. Subtract from race length.
