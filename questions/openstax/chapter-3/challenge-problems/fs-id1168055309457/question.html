A motorcycle's maximum speed is {{params.vmax}} km/h on a {{params.course}} km course. It reaches {{params.benchmark}} km/h from rest in {{params.tb}} s at constant acceleration, continues that acceleration to maximum speed, then cruises. Find total course time.

Convert both speeds to m/s. Acceleration is benchmark speed divided by benchmark time. Then t_max=v_max/a and acceleration distance=(1/2)at_max^2. Cruise the remaining course at v_max.
