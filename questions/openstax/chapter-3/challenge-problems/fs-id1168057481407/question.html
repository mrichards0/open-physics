A cyclist is {{params.d}} m from the finish, initially at {{params.vi}} m/s. She accelerates at {{params.a}} m/s^2 for {{params.ta}} s, then holds that final speed. A rival starts {{params.gap}} m ahead at constant {{params.vr}} m/s.

Find (a) winner's final speed, (b) time saved versus staying at initial speed, (c) difference in finish times, and (d) how far behind the rival is when the winner finishes.

Compute distance during acceleration, then cruise time for the remaining distance. Compare this total with d/vi for time saved. The rival travels d-gap at constant speed; finish-time difference times rival speed equals the rival's remaining distance when the winner finishes.
