A particle has x(t)={{params.a}}t^2-{{params.b}}t^3 meters for t>=0.

(a) Write v(t)=c1 t+c2 t^2 and acceleration a(t)=d0+d1 t; find the coefficients. (b) Evaluate v and acceleration at t={{params.te}} s. (c) Find the positive time of maximum position, (d) the positive zero-velocity time, and (e) maximum position.

Differentiate: v=2at-3bt^2=t(2a-3bt), and acceleration=2a-6bt. Besides t=0, velocity vanishes at 2a/(3b); this is the positive position maximum. Substitute it into x(t).
