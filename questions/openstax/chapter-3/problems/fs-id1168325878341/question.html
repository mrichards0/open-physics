A rock falls from rest from height {{params.h}} m. A hiker first sees it {{params.delay}} s after it breaks loose. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find (a) the rock's height then and (b) remaining time before impact.

After the delay it has fallen (1/2)g delay^2. Total fall time is sqrt(2h/g); subtract the elapsed delay.
