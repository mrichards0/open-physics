A ball is thrown upward at {{params.v0}} m/s and passes a branch {{params.h}} m above release on the way up. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. How much additional time elapses before it passes the branch downward?

Solve h=v0t-(1/2)gt^2. The two roots are the upward and downward passage times; their difference is 2sqrt(v0^2-2gh)/g.
