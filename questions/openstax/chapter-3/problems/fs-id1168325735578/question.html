A ball is thrown straight upward from y=0 at {{params.v0}} m/s. Take upward positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find displacement and signed velocity at t={{params.t1}}, {{params.t2}}, {{params.t3}}, and {{params.t4}} s.

At each time use y=v0t-(1/2)gt^2 and v=v0-gt. Negative velocity means downward motion.
