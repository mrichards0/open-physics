A train's top speed is {{params.kmh}} km/h. (a) Starting from rest at {{params.a1}} m/s^2, find time to top speed. (b) Braking normally at magnitude {{params.a2}} m/s^2, find stopping time. (c) It can stop from top speed in {{params.te}} s in an emergency; take forward positive and find signed emergency acceleration.

Convert top speed by dividing km/h by 3.6. Use t=Delta v/a for (a,b), and a=(0-v)/te for the signed emergency acceleration.
