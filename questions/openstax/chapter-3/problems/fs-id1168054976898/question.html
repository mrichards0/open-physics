For t>=1 s, velocity is v(t)=A+B/t with A={{params.A}} m/s and B={{params.B}} m, and x(1 s)=0. Find acceleration and position at t={{params.t1}} s and t={{params.t2}} s.

Differentiate to get a=-B/t^2. Integrating from 1 s to t gives x=A(t-1)+B ln(t/1 s).
