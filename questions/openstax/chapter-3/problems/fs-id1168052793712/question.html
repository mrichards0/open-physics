A particle has a(t)=p t^2-q t^3 with v(0)=x(0)=0.

Write v(t)=c3 p t^3+c4 q t^4 and x(t)=d4 p t^4+d5 q t^5. Find all four signed coefficients. Then evaluate v and x for p={{params.p}}, q={{params.q}}, and t={{params.t}} in consistent SI units.

Integrate term by term: v=(p/3)t^3-(q/4)t^4. Integrating again gives x=(p/12)t^4-(q/20)t^5; the zero initial conditions remove both constants.
