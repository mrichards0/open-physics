A care package impacts at {{params.v}} m/s and stops uniformly through {{params.d}} m of trees and snow. Taking the impact direction as positive, find its acceleration.

From 0=v^2+2a Delta x, a=-v^2/(2 Delta x). The negative sign indicates acceleration opposite the impact velocity.
