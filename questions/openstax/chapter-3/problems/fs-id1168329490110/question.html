Take a traffic light as x=0 and east as positive. At t=0 a car is {{params.west}} km west of the light; at t={{params.time}} min it is {{params.east}} km east.

Find both signed positions and the displacement.

West is negative and east positive. Displacement is x_f-x_0, so subtracting a negative initial position adds its magnitude.
