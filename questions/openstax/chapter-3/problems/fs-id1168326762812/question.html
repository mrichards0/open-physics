An object's position is x(t)={{params.x0}}+{{params.v}}t meters. Find its velocity and identify the correct description of the x-t and v-t graphs.

x(t) is a straight line with slope v; v(t) is a horizontal line at v.Both graphs are parabolas.x(t) is horizontal and v(t) has slope v.Both graphs are vertical lines.

Differentiating x0+vt gives the constant velocity v. Linear position has constant slope, so its velocity graph is horizontal.
