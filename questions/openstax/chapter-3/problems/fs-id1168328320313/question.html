A rocket sled accelerates from rest to {{params.v}} m/s in {{params.ta}} s, then brakes to rest in {{params.tb}} s. Take forward as positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find both signed accelerations and each signed value in multiples of g.

Use Delta v/Delta t for each phase. Braking has Delta v=0-v, hence a negative sign; divide each acceleration by g for g-units.
