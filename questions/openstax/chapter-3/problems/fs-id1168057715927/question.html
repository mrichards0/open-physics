A rocket has a(t)=A-B t^(1/2), starts from rest at x=0, and is evaluated at t={{params.t}} s with A={{params.A}} and B={{params.B}} in consistent SI units.

Select the units of A and B.

A: m/s^2; B: m/s^(5/2)A: m/s; B: m/s^2A: m/s^2; B: m/s^(3/2)

B times sqrt(t) must have acceleration units, so B has m/s^(5/2). Integration gives v=At-(2/3)Bt^(3/2) and x=(1/2)At^2-(4/15)Bt^(5/2).
