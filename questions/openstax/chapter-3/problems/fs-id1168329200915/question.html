A player moving at {{params.v}} m/s compresses padding by {{params.d}} m while stopping uniformly. Taking the initial direction as positive, find (a) acceleration and (b) collision duration.

Use 0=v^2+2a Delta x, then the average velocity v/2 gives t=2 Delta x/v.
