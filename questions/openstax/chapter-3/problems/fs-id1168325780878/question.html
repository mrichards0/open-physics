A boulder falls from rest down a {{params.h}} m cliff. Sound from the break travels at {{params.sound}} m/s to a tourist below, whose reaction time is {{params.reaction}} s. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Find (a) impact speed and (b) time available to move after hearing the sound and reacting.

Impact speed is sqrt(2gh), and fall time is sqrt(2h/g). The warning is delayed by h/sound; subtract that and reaction time from fall time.
