A particle follows x(t)={{params.c}}t-{{params.k}}t^2 meters. At t1={{params.t1}} s and t2={{params.t2}} s, find both instantaneous velocities, both speeds, and the average velocity over the interval.

Instantaneous velocity is v=c-2kt and speed is |v|. Average velocity is [x(t2)-x(t1)]/(t2-t1).
