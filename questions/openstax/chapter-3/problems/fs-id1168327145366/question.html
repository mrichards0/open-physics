A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly at {{params.a}} m/s^2 for {{params.t}} s.

Which data are known?

v0=0, acceleration a, and elapsed time t.Final position, final velocity, and mass.Only acceleration; time is unknown.

Which equations directly find displacement and final velocity?

Delta x=v0t+(1/2)at^2 and v=v0+at.Delta x=at and v=v0t.Delta x=v^2/a and v=a/t.

With v0=0, displacement is (1/2)at^2 and final velocity is at. The units reduce to meters and meters per second, respectively.
