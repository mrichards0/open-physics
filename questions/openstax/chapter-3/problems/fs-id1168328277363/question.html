A rock is thrown straight down from a bridge at {{params.speed}} m/s. The bridge is {{params.height}} m high. Take upward positive, y=0 at release, and g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find signed displacement and velocity at t={{params.t1}}, {{params.t2}}, {{params.t3}}, {{params.t4}}, and {{params.t5}} s.

Here v0 is negative and a=-g. Use y=-v_initial t-(1/2)gt^2 and v=-v_initial-gt. Generated times occur before impact.
