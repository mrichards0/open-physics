A particle starts from rest at x=0. For 0<=t<=T1={{params.T1}} s, v={{params.k1}}t. From T1 to T2={{params.T2}} s, v={{params.vpeak}}-{{params.k2}}(t-T1). After T2, velocity stays at {{params.vfinal}} m/s.

Find acceleration in all three intervals and position at t={{params.ta}}, {{params.tb}}, and {{params.tc}} s.

Acceleration is each velocity segment's slope: k1, -k2, and 0. Position is accumulated area under v(t): a triangle in the first interval, a trapezoid in the second, then a rectangle at constant velocity.
