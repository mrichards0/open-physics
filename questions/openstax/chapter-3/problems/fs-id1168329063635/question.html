A blast occurs at altitude {{params.altitude}} km. Its wave reaches an observer after {{params.seconds}} s along a straight path making {{params.angle}} degrees with the horizontal. Find (a) average speed and (b) the ratio to a {{params.sound}} m/s speed of sound.

The altitude is the vertical component L sin(theta), so path length L=h/sin(theta). Divide by elapsed time, then by the sound speed.
