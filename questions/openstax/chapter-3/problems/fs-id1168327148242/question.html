At t={{params.t1}} s a particle moves right at {{params.v1}} m/s. At t={{params.t2}} s it moves left at {{params.speed2}} m/s. With constant acceleration and right positive, find (a) acceleration, (b) velocity at t=0, and (c) the time its velocity is zero.

The second signed velocity is negative. Compute a=(v2-v1)/(t2-t1), extrapolate v0=v1-at1, and solve 0=v0+at.
