A particle's position is x(t)={{params.x0}}+({{params.v}})t meters.

(a) When does it cross the origin? (b) What is its displacement from t={{params.t1}} s to t={{params.t2}} s?

Set x=0 to obtain t=-x0/v. For constant velocity, displacement over the interval is v(t2-t1).
