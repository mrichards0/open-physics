A life preserver is thrown straight down at {{params.v0}} m/s and reaches the water in {{params.t}} s. Use downward positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Select the correct knowns, then find release height.

v0, elapsed time, and downward acceleration g are known; displacement is unknown.Release height and final velocity are known; time is unknown.Only mass and gravity are known.

With downward positive, height=v0t+(1/2)gt^2.
