Take right as positive. An animal runs {{params.right}} m right in {{params.tr}} s, then {{params.left}} m left in {{params.tl}} s. Find average velocity and average speed.

Average velocity uses signed displacement (right-left); average speed uses total distance (right+left). Both divide by total time.
