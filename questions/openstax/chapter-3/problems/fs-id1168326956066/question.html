A particle starts with velocity {{params.v0}} m/s and constant positive acceleration {{params.a}} m/s^2. (a) At what positive time is its displacement {{params.dx}} m? (b) What is its velocity then?

Solve (1/2)at^2+v0t-Delta x=0 and keep the positive root. Then use v=v0+at.
