A woodpecker head stops uniformly from {{params.v}} m/s over {{params.d1}} mm. Its brain is cushioned over {{params.d2}} mm. Take the initial direction as positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Find head acceleration, head acceleration/g, stopping time, and brain acceleration/g.

For each stopping distance, 0=v^2+2a Delta x, so a=-v^2/(2 Delta x). Head stopping time is 2 Delta x/v, and division by g gives signed g-units.
