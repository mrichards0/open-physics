A rock thrown upward from a cliff at {{params.v0}} m/s reaches the ground {{params.tup}} s later. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. (a) Find cliff height. (b) Find the time to ground if thrown downward at the same speed.

For the upward throw, -h=v0t-(1/2)gt^2, so h=(1/2)gt^2-v0t. For a downward throw, h=v0t+(1/2)gt^2; keep its positive root.
