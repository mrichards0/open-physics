A diver takes off upward at {{params.v0}} m/s from a board {{params.h}} m above the water. Take upward positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find (a) maximum height above the board, (b) total time until her feet reach water, and (c) signed impact velocity.

Rise is v0^2/(2g). Solve -h=v0t-(1/2)gt^2 for the positive time, then use v=v0-gt; the impact velocity is negative.
