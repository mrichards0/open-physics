A train enters a {{params.station}} m station at {{params.v0}} m/s and accelerates at -{{params.decel}} m/s^2. The train is {{params.length}} m long. Find (a) nose speed and (b) elapsed time when the nose exits; then (c) rear speed and (d) elapsed time when the rear exits.

For each point use v^2=v0^2+2a Delta x and t=(v-v0)/a. The nose travels the station length; the rear must travel station length plus train length.
