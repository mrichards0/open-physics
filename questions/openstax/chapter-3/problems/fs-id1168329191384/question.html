A velocity-versus-time graph is piecewise constant: v={{params.v1}} m/s for {{params.dt1}} s, then {{params.v2}} m/s for {{params.dt2}} s, then {{params.v3}} m/s for {{params.dt3}} s. The initial position is {{params.x0}} m.

Find the displacement of each segment and final position, then identify the position-graph shape.

Connected straight segments with slopes v1, v2, and v3; position stays continuous at each breakpoint.Three disconnected horizontal segments at heights v1, v2, and v3.A graph with vertical jumps equal to each velocity change.One straight segment whose slope is the sum v1+v2+v3.

Displacement is the signed area under the velocity graph, vi Delta ti. Accumulating those areas produces a continuous, piecewise-linear position graph.
