A particle has x(t)={{params.a}}t^3+{{params.b}}t meters for t>=0. Find its minimum velocity and decide whether its velocity can equal zero.

No. v(t)=3at^2+b is positive for every real t because a and b are positive.Yes, at t=b/(3a).Yes, at t=sqrt(b/(3a)).Yes, at t=0 because position is zero there.

Differentiate: v=3at^2+b. Its smallest value occurs at t=0 and equals b, so setting it to zero would require a negative t^2 and has no real solution.
