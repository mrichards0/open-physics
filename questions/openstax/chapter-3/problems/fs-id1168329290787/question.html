A train starts at {{params.v0}} m/s and accelerates at {{params.a}} m/s^2 for {{params.minutes}} min. It then brakes at acceleration magnitude {{params.brake}} m/s^2 until stopping.

Find final speed after acceleration, braking time, distance during acceleration, and braking distance.

Convert minutes to seconds. Use vf=v0+at and average velocity for the first distance. During braking, t=vf/b and distance=vf^2/(2b).
