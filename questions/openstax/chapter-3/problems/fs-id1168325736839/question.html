A kangaroo's vertical trajectory reaches {{params.h}} m above takeoff. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find (a) upward takeoff speed and (b) total air time back to takeoff height.

At the apex v=0, so v0=sqrt(2gh). The symmetric up-and-down time is 2v0/g.
