A car starts from rest and accelerates backward at {{params.a}} m/s^2 until its speed is {{params.v}} m/s. (a) How long does this take? It then brakes to rest in {{params.tb}} s. Taking backward as positive, (b) find the braking acceleration.

During startup, t=v/a. During braking, acceleration=(0-v)/tb, negative because it opposes the chosen positive backward direction.
