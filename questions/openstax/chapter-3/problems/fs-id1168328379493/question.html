A dolphin leaves the water straight upward at {{params.v0}} m/s. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2 and neglect air resistance. Find maximum rise and total time in the air.

At maximum height, which quantity is known?

Instantaneous vertical velocity is zero.Acceleration is zero.Both velocity and acceleration are zero.

At the apex v=0 but acceleration remains -g. Thus h=v0^2/(2g). Symmetry gives total time 2v0/g.
