A shot is released straight upward at {{params.v0}} m/s from height {{params.release}} m. The athlete's height is {{params.person}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. How long until the descending shot returns to head height?

Relative to release, head height is y=-(release-person). Solve y=v0t-(1/2)gt^2 and choose the later positive root: [v0+sqrt(v0^2+2g(release-person))]/g.
