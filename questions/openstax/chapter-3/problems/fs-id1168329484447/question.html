A runner moves forward at {{params.v0}} m/s and is assigned constant acceleration -{{params.decel}} m/s^2 for {{params.t}} s. (a) Find signed displacement and (b) final velocity from that model. (c) Evaluate the result.

The negative final velocity means the constant-acceleration model makes her stop and reverse; if she only slows to a stop, the model is invalid after stopping.A negative velocity is impossible, so the kinematic equations cannot be used at all.The result means she continues forward at the same speed.

Use Delta x=v0t+(1/2)at^2 and v=v0+at. Here t exceeds v0/deceleration, so the mathematical model continues through zero velocity into reverse motion.
