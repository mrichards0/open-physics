Take east as positive. A cyclist rides {{params.west}} km west, reverses direction, and rides {{params.east}} km east. Find (a) signed displacement, (b) distance traveled, and (c) displacement magnitude.

Displacement is final position minus initial position: -d_west+d_east. Distance adds path lengths, while displacement magnitude is the absolute value of signed displacement.
