An airplane is moving east while landing and slowing down. Which statement correctly describes its acceleration during the slowdown?

It points west, opposite the eastward velocity.It points east because the airplane is still moving east.It is zero because the airplane has not reversed direction.It points vertically downward because the airplane is landing.

Acceleration describes change in velocity. Reducing an eastward velocity requires a westward acceleration component.
