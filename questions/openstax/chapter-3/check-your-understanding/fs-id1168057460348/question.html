A particle starts from rest at x=0 with acceleration a(t)={{params.a0}}-{{params.b}}t in m/s^2.

(a) Express v(t)=c1 t+c2 t^2 and find c1,c2. (b) Express x(t)=d2 t^2+d3 t^3 and find d2,d3. (c) Find the positive time when velocity is again zero.

Integrate acceleration and apply v(0)=0: v=a0 t-(b/2)t^2. Integrate again and apply x(0)=0: x=(a0/2)t^2-(b/6)t^3. Factoring v=t(a0-bt/2) gives the positive zero t=2a0/b.
