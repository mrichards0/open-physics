An object's position is x(t)=-{{params.k}}t^2 m, with t in seconds.

(a) Write v(t)=c t and find c. (b) Can v be positive for t>0? (c) Find velocity and speed at t={{params.t}} s.

No; c is negative and t is positive.Yes; speed is always positive, so velocity must be positive.Yes; differentiating changes the sign.

Differentiate x: v=dx/dt=-2kt. For positive t this is negative; speed is |v|.
