Ice falls from rest through {{params.h}} m with negligible air resistance. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

(a) Find the fall time. (b) During the fall, which grows faster with time: speed or distance traveled?

Distance; it grows as t^2, while speed grows as t.Speed; it grows as t^2, while distance grows as t.They grow at the same rate because acceleration is constant.

From h=(1/2)gt^2, t=sqrt(2h/g). Also v=gt is linear in time, while h=(1/2)gt^2 is quadratic.
