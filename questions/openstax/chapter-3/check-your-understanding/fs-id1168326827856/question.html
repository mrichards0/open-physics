A bicycle passes a runner at constant speed {{params.v}} m/s. At that instant the runner starts from rest with constant acceleration and catches the bicycle {{params.t}} s later. Find the runner's acceleration.

At the catch, both displacements equal: v_b t=(1/2)at^2. Solving gives a=2v_b/t.
