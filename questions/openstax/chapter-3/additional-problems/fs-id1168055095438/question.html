An airplane accelerates uniformly at {{params.a}} m/s^2 for {{params.t}} s while covering {{params.km}} km. Find initial and final velocity.

Convert distance to meters. From Delta x=v0t+(1/2)at^2, v0=[Delta x-(1/2)at^2]/t. Then vf=v0+at.
