A car passes a stationary police car at constant speed {{params.v}} m/s. At that instant police accelerate from rest at {{params.a}} m/s^2. How long until police catch the car?

Equal positions require (1/2)at^2=vt. Besides t=0, the catch time is t=2v/a.
