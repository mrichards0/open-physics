An electron enters a {{params.cm}} cm region at {{params.v0}} m/s and accelerates in its direction of motion at {{params.a}} m/s^2. Find (a) exit velocity and (b) crossing time.

Convert length to meters. Use vf=sqrt(v0^2+2aL), then t=(vf-v0)/a.
