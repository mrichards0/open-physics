Pablo runs at {{params.v}} m/s. Jacob is {{params.gap}} m behind at the same speed, then accelerates at {{params.a}} m/s^2. Find (a) catch time, (b) Jacob's distance during that time, and (c) Jacob's final speed.

Initial equal speeds cancel in relative motion, so gap=(1/2)at^2. Then Jacob's distance is vt+(1/2)at^2 and final speed is v+at.
