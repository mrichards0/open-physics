An object has constant acceleration {{params.a}} cm/s^2. At t={{params.tk}} s its velocity is {{params.vk}} cm/s. Find velocity at t={{params.t1}} s and t={{params.t2}} s.

Relative to the known instant, v(t)=v_known+a(t-t_known).
