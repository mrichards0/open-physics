A ball is dropped from {{params.hdrop}} m and rebounds to {{params.hrebound}} m. Floor contact lasts {{params.ms}} ms. Take upward positive and use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find velocity just before impact, velocity just after, average contact acceleration, and maximum compression assuming constant contact acceleration and a rigid floor.

Speeds follow sqrt(2gh), with negative sign before and positive after. Average contact acceleration is (v_after-v_before)/Delta t. Compression ends when velocity first reaches zero, giving magnitude v_before^2/(2a).
