A cyclist starts from rest, accelerates at {{params.a1}} m/s^2 for {{params.t1}} min, travels at the resulting constant speed for {{params.t2}} min, then slows uniformly to rest over {{params.t3}} min.

Find final-phase acceleration and total distance, then identify the velocity graph.
