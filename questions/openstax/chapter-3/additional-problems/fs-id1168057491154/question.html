Two planes travel the same {{params.distance}} km displacement. Plane 1 takes {{params.t1}} h. Plane 2 departs {{params.delay}} h later but arrives with plane 1. Find both average velocities and their ratio v2/v1.

Plane 2's travel time is t1-delay. Divide the common displacement by each travel time; the shorter-time plane has larger average velocity.
