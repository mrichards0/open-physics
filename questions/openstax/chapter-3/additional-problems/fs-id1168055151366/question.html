A {{params.lt}} m truck at {{params.vt}} km/h passes a {{params.lc}} m car at {{params.vc}} km/h in the same direction. Find the elapsed time from truck-front aligned with car-rear until truck-rear aligned with car-front.

The truck must gain the sum of both lengths relative to the car. Relative speed is (vt-vc)/3.6 in m/s, so time=(Lt+Lc)/v_relative.
