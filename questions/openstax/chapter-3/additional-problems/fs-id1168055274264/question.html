A balloon rises from ground at constant {{params.v0}} m/s. After {{params.release}} s it drops a sandbag. Take upward positive and use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find (a) time from release to ground and (b) signed impact velocity.

At release, height=v0 times release time and the bag still has upward velocity v0. Solve 0=h+v0t-(1/2)gt^2, then v=v0-gt.
