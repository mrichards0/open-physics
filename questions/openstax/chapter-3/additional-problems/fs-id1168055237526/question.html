A ball thrown upward passes a window whose bottom is {{params.bottom}} m above ground and whose height is {{params.window}} m. It takes {{params.dt}} s to pass the window upward. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find launch speed from ground.

Across the window, h=v_bottom Delta t-(1/2)g Delta t^2, so v_bottom=[h+(1/2)g Delta t^2]/Delta t. Relate bottom speed to launch speed with v_bottom^2=v0^2-2g y_bottom.
