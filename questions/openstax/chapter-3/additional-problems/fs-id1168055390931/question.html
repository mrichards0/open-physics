A train moves uphill at constant velocity when its caboose breaks free with the same instantaneous velocity. After {{params.t}} s, the caboose is {{params.behind}} m behind the train. Take uphill as positive. Find the caboose's acceleration.

Relative to the constant-velocity train, initial relative velocity is zero and relative displacement is -d=(1/2)at^2. Thus a=-2d/t^2.
