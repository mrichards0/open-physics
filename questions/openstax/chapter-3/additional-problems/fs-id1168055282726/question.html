A coin is released from a balloon at height {{params.h}} m while moving upward at {{params.v0}} m/s. Take upward positive and g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find (a) maximum height above ground, (b) position and signed velocity {{params.teval}} s after release, and (c) time to ground.

The coin retains the balloon's upward velocity. Its additional rise is v0^2/(2g). Use y=h+v0t-(1/2)gt^2 and v=v0-gt; solve y=0 for the positive impact root.
