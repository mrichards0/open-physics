A motorcycle slowing uniformly covers two successive equal distances of {{params.km}} km in {{params.t1}} s and {{params.t2}} s. Find (a) acceleration, (b) velocity at the start of the first segment, and (c) velocity at the end of the second.

Each segment's average velocity is L/t. Their difference equals a(t1+t2)/2, so a=2L(1/t2-1/t1)/(t1+t2). Then v_initial=L/t1-at1/2 and v_final=v_initial+a(t1+t2).
