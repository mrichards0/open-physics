Two identical trains approach each other at {{params.v}} m/s each and begin equal-magnitude braking when {{params.d}} m apart. What signed acceleration must each have to stop just before collision? Take each train's initial direction as positive.

By symmetry each train travels d/2. From 0=v^2+2a(d/2), a=-v^2/d.
