Two objects start at the same speed {{params.v0}} m/s and accelerate constantly for {{params.t}} s. Object 1 changes velocity by 2v0; object 2 changes velocity by 4v0. Find each distance and ratio d2/d1.

Final speeds are 3v0 and 5v0. Constant-acceleration average speeds are therefore 2v0 and 3v0, giving distances 2v0t and 3v0t and ratio 3/2.
