A sprinter starts from rest, accelerates uniformly for {{params.ta}} s to maximum speed, then maintains it. For a {{params.d1}} m race completed in {{params.T1}} s, find maximum speed and acceleration. Under the same acceleration-duration assumption, find maximum speed for a {{params.d2}} m race completed in {{params.T2}} s.

Distance is triangle plus rectangle area under v(t): D=(1/2)vmax ta+vmax(T-ta)=vmax(T-ta/2). Thus vmax=D/(T-ta/2), and a=vmax/ta.
