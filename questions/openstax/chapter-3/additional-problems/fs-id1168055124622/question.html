An ambulance travels at {{params.kmh}} km/h and must cover {{params.d}} m in {{params.t}} s. Assuming constant acceleration, find (a) the minimum acceleration and (b) arrival speed.

Convert initial speed to m/s. From d=v0t+(1/2)at^2, a=2(d-v0t)/t^2. Then vf=v0+at.
