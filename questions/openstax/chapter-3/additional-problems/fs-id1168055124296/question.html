Two players return to their takeoff heights after jumping vertically by {{params.h1}} m and {{params.h2}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find each air time and the ratio t1/t2.

For maximum height h, v0=sqrt(2gh) and total time is 2v0/g. Therefore the time ratio is sqrt(h1/h2).
