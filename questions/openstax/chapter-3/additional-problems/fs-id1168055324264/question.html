An object is dropped from a building. During its final one second, it falls 1/{{params.f}} of the total height. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find total fall time and building height.

The final-second distance is g(T-1/2), while total height is (1/2)gT^2. Setting g(T-1/2)=h/f gives T^2-2fT+f=0; choose the root greater than one second.
