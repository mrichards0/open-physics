An object is dropped from {{params.h}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find distance during the first second, signed impact velocity (upward positive), and distance during the final one second before impact.

Total fall time is T=sqrt(2h/g). First-second distance is g/2. Impact velocity is -gT. Last-second distance is h-(1/2)g(T-1)^2.
