An object is dropped and falls for {{params.t}} s. Earth gravity is {{params.g}} m/s^2; lunar gravity is {{params.fraction}} of Earth's. Find distance fallen on Earth and Moon.

From rest, distance=(1/2)gt^2. Replace g by the stated fraction for the Moon.
