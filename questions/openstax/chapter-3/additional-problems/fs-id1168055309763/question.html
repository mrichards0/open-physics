Take east as positive. A cyclist rides {{params.e1}} km east, {{params.w1}} km west, {{params.e2}} km east, {{params.w2}} km west, and {{params.e3}} km east. Someone reports a positive average velocity of {{params.average}} km/h.

Using that report, calculate the implied time and evaluate it.

The negative time is unreasonable: the displacement is westward, so average velocity must be negative, not positive.The negative time means the cyclist traveled backward through time.The result is reasonable because westward distance is always positive.

Net displacement is the signed sum. Since Delta t=Delta x/v_avg, opposite signs for displacement and reported velocity produce an impossible negative elapsed time.
