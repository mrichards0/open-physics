A runner completes 10,000 m on a 400 m closed track and finishes at the starting line. What is the net displacement?

Zero; 25 complete laps return the runner to the initial position.10,000 m in the running direction.400 m because that is one lap.25 m because 25 laps were completed.

Displacement depends only on initial and final positions, not total distance.
