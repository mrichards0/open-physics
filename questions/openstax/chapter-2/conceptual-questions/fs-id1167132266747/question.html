If three nonzero vectors sum to zero, what geometric condition do they satisfy?

Placed head-to-tail, they form a closed triangle.They must all be parallel in the same direction.They must all have equal magnitudes.They must be mutually perpendicular.

A zero resultant means the head-to-tail path returns to its starting point.
