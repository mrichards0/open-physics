Can a vector's magnitude be negative?

No; magnitude is a nonnegative length computed from squared components.Yes, when the vector points in a negative coordinate direction.Yes, whenever one component is negative.Only for displacement vectors.

Components may be signed, but sqrt(A_x^2+A_y^2+A_z^2) is never negative.
