Does an automobile odometer indicate a scalar or vector?

Scalar; it accumulates total distance traveled.Vector; it records displacement from the starting point.Vector; the car has a direction of travel.Neither, because distance has units.

An odometer does not retain direction, so it measures path length rather than displacement.
