Which listed quantity is a vector?

The velocity of a flyA person's heightThe age of EarthThe boiling point of water

Which other listed quantity is a vector?

The acceleration of gravityThe cost of a bookEarth's populationAltitude on Mount Everest

Velocity and acceleration require magnitude and direction. The other listed quantities are scalars.
