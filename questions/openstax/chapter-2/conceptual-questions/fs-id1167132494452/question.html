Why can no Cartesian component have magnitude greater than the vector's magnitude?

Because |A|=sqrt(A_x^2+A_y^2+A_z^2), which is at least as large as the absolute value of each component.Because every component must be positive.Because all components always have equal magnitude.Because components have no units.

The sum of nonnegative squared components includes each individual square.
