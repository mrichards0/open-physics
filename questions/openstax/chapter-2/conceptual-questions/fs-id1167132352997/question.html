For vectors with magnitudes A and B, which relative directions give the largest and smallest possible magnitude of A+B?

Largest when parallel: A+B. Smallest when antiparallel: |A-B|.Largest when perpendicular: sqrt(A^2+B^2). Smallest when parallel: A+B.Largest when antiparallel: |A-B|. Smallest when parallel: A+B.Both extrema occur when perpendicular.

The triangle inequality gives |A-B| <= |A+B| <= A+B, with equality for antiparallel and parallel directions.
