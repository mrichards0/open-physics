If A cross B=0, what can be concluded about nonzero A and B?

They are parallel or antiparallel.They are perpendicular.They must have equal magnitude.They point at 45 degrees to each other.

|A cross B|=AB sin(theta), so a zero result for nonzero vectors requires theta=0 or 180 degrees.
