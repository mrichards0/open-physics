How are vectors and scalars alike and different?

Both have magnitude and units; only vectors additionally have direction and obey vector addition.Only vectors have units.Scalars have direction but vectors do not.They differ only in whether their numerical value is positive.

Both describe measurable quantities, but spatial direction changes how vectors combine.
