If nonzero vectors A and B are orthogonal, what is each vector's scalar component along the other?

Both projections are zero.Both projections equal one.Each projection equals the vector's full magnitude.The projections are undefined.

Orthogonality means A dot B=0, so projection A dot unit(B) and projection B dot unit(A) both vanish.
