A forecast temperature of -5 degrees C is what kind of quantity?

Scalar; it has a value and unit but no spatial direction.Vector; its negative sign specifies a direction.Vector; every measured quantity is a vector.Neither scalar nor vector because it is below zero.

A minus sign on a scalar indicates a value below the chosen zero, not a direction in space.
