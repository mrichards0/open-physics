A polar bear has velocity ({{params.x}} i + {{params.y}} j) km/h, where i is east and j is north. It is traveling southwest. Find its speed and its direction in degrees south of west.

Speed is sqrt(vx^2+vy^2). In the southwest quadrant, the angle south of west is atan2(|vy|,|vx|).
