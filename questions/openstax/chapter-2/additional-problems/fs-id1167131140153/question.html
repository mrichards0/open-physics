A parallelepiped has edge vectors A=({{params.ax}},{{params.ay}},{{params.az}}), B=({{params.bx}},{{params.by}},{{params.bz}}), and C=({{params.cx}},{{params.cy}},{{params.cz}}). Find its volume.

|B cross C| is base area, and dotting with A supplies the perpendicular height; absolute value makes volume nonnegative.B cross C is the base perimeter, and dotting adds the three edge lengths.A dot B is the base area, and crossing with C gives a scalar height.

The scalar triple product gives signed base-area times height, so V=|(B cross C) dot A|.
