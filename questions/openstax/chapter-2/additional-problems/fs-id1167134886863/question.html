For G=({{params.gx}},{{params.gy}},{{params.gz}}) and H=({{params.hx}},{{params.hy}},{{params.hz}}), find (a) all components of G cross H, (b) its magnitude, and (c) G dot H.

Use the determinant formula for G cross H, its Euclidean magnitude for part (b), and sum of component products for part (c).
