Read vectors G and H from the generated three-dimensional figure. Find (a) all components of G cross H, (b) its magnitude, and (c) G dot H.

First resolve both vectors using x=R cos(beta)cos(alpha), y=R cos(beta)sin(alpha), and z=R sin(beta). Then use the determinant formula for G cross H and the component-product sum for G dot H.
