Four forces each have magnitude {{params.f}} N. Find the largest and smallest possible resultant magnitudes, then select an arrangement producing each extreme.

Largest:

All four forces point in the same direction.The forces form two opposite pairs.The forces point at 90-degree intervals.

Smallest:

The forces form opposite pairs, so their vector sum is zero.All four forces point in the same direction.Three point together and one points oppositely.

Aligned equal vectors add to 4f. A closed arrangement—such as two opposite pairs—adds to zero, the smallest possible magnitude.
