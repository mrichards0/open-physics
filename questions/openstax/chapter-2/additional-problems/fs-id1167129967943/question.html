A diver swims {{params.north}} m north, {{params.east}} m east, then {{params.grouper}} m at {{params.angle}} degrees north of east. A current also displaces her {{params.current}} m south. The current stops. Find the distance and direction she must swim to return, expressed as degrees north of west.

Add all east/north components. The return vector is the negative of that resultant. Its magnitude gives the distance, and atan2(north return component, west return component) gives the angle north of west.
