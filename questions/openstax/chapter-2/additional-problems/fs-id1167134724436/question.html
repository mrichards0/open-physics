A=({{params.ax}},{{params.ay}}) lies in the xy-plane. Find the vector B with the same magnitude, orthogonal to A, and with B_y positive.

Rotating (Ax,Ay) counterclockwise by 90 degrees gives (-Ay,Ax). It has the same magnitude, its dot product with A is zero, and here Ax is positive so By is positive.
