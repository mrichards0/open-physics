A point P has polar coordinates (r={{params.r}}, phi={{params.phi}} degrees). Give the polar radius and standard 0-to-360-degree angle for:

(a) (-x,y), (b) (-2x,-2y), and (c) (3x,-3y), where (x,y) are P's rectangular coordinates.

Reflecting x changes the angle to 180 degrees minus phi; multiplying both coordinates by -2 reverses direction and doubles radius; multiplying x and y by 3 and -3 reflects across the x-axis and triples radius. Normalize angles to 0–360 degrees.
