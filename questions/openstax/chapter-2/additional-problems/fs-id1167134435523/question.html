Plane 1 is at altitude {{params.h1}} m and horizontal range {{params.r1}} km, {{params.a1}} degrees south of west. Plane 2 is at altitude {{params.h2}} m and horizontal range {{params.r2}} km, {{params.a2}} degrees south of west. Find their straight-line separation.

Resolve each horizontal range into east/north components, convert the altitude difference to kilometers, subtract the two position vectors, and take the 3-D magnitude.
