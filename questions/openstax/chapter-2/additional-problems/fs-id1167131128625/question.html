A skater follows a circle of radius {{params.r}} m. Find (a) displacement magnitude after half a circle, (b) distance traveled after half a circle, and (c) displacement magnitude after a complete circle.

Opposite points are separated by the diameter 2r, while the half-circle path length is pi r. A complete trip returns to the starting point, so displacement is zero.
