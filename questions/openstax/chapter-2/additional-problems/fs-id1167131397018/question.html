An aircraft flies {{params.distance}} km at {{params.angle}} degrees south of west.

(a) Resolve the displacement into distances due south and due west.

(b) Resolve it into signed components along axes pointing 45 degrees south of west and 45 degrees west of north.

On east/north axes the vector is (-d cos(theta), -d sin(theta)). Dotting it with the two rotated unit vectors gives the signed rotated-axis components.
