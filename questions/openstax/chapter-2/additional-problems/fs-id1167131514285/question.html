Vectors A and B have magnitudes {{params.a}} and {{params.b}}, with angle {{params.phi}} degrees between them. If C=A+B, calculate |C| and identify the correct squared-magnitude identity.

C^2=A^2+B^2+2AB cos(phi)C^2=A^2+B^2-2AB cos(phi)C=A+B+2AB cos(phi)C^2=A^2+B^2+AB sin(phi)

Dot C=A+B with itself: C dot C=A dot A+B dot B+2A dot B. Since A dot B=AB cos(phi), the displayed identity follows.
