A leash pulls with force F=({{params.x}},{{params.y}},{{params.z}}) N. Find its magnitude and its angle with the positive vertical z-axis.

|F| is the square root of the sum of squared components. The direction angle with vertical satisfies cos(theta)=Fz/|F|.
