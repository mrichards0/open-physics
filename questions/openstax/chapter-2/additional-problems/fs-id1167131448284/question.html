Vectors A and B each have magnitude {{params.magnitude}} units, and |A+B|={{params.resultant}} units. Find the smaller angle between A and B.

Square A+B: R^2=A^2+B^2+2AB cos(theta). Thus cos(theta)=(R^2-2M^2)/(2M^2).
