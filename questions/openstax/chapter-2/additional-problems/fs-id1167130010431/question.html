Vector G has magnitude {{params.gmag}}, azimuth {{params.gaz}} degrees counterclockwise from +x, and elevation {{params.gel}} degrees above the xy-plane. Vector H has magnitude {{params.hmag}}, azimuth {{params.haz}} degrees, and elevation {{params.hel}} degrees. Find their Cartesian components.

For magnitude R, azimuth alpha, and elevation beta: x=R cos(beta)cos(alpha), y=R cos(beta)sin(alpha), z=R sin(beta).
