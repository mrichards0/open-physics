The figure gives the magnitude, azimuth, and elevation of vectors G and H. Read each vector from the figure and find its Cartesian components.

For magnitude R, azimuth alpha, and elevation beta: x=R cos(beta)cos(alpha), y=R cos(beta)sin(alpha), z=R sin(beta).
