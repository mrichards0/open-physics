For vectors A=({{params.ax}}, {{params.ay}}, {{params.az}}), F=({{params.fx}}, {{params.fy}}, {{params.fz}}), D=({{params.dx}}, {{params.dy}}, {{params.dz}}), B=({{params.bx}}, {{params.by}}, {{params.bz}}), calculate (a) (A cross F) dot D, (b) (A cross F) dot (D cross B), and all components of (c) (A dot F)(D cross B).

Compute each cross product component first. Part (a) is a scalar triple product; part (b) is a dot product of two vectors; part (c) scales D cross B by the scalar A dot F.
