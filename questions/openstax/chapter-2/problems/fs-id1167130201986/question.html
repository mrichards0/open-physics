All vectors lie in the xy-plane: A=({{params.ax}}, {{params.ay}}, {{params.az}}), C=({{params.cx}}, {{params.cy}}, {{params.cz}}), F=({{params.fx}}, {{params.fy}}, {{params.fz}}), B=({{params.bx}}, {{params.by}}, {{params.bz}}), D=({{params.dx}}, {{params.dy}}, {{params.dz}}). Give the signed z-component of each cross product.

For planar vectors, (U cross V)z=UxVy-UyVx. Cross products are distributive; a vector crossed with itself is zero.
