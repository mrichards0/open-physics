A horizontal force is F=({{params.fx}}, {{params.fy}}) N, with +x east and +y north. Find magnitude and direction counterclockwise from east.

|F|=sqrt(F_x^2+F_y^2)={{correct_answers.magnitude}} N, and atan2(F_y,F_x) gives {{correct_answers.angle}} degrees.
