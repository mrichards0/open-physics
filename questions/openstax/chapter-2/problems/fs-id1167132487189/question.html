You drive {{params.distance}} km at {{params.angle}} degrees east of north. Find equivalent east and north legs.

Reversing the order of the east and north legs reaches the same endpoint.Reversing the legs changes the endpoint because vector addition is not commutative.Reversing the legs changes both component magnitudes.

For an angle east of north, east=d sin(theta)={{correct_answers.east}} km and north=d cos(theta)={{correct_answers.north}} km. Component addition is commutative, so order does not affect the endpoint.
