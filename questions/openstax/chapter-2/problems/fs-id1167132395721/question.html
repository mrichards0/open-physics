A surveyor walks {{params.baseline}} m along a straight river from a point opposite a tree. The sightline back to the tree makes angle {{params.angle}} degrees with the baseline. Find river width.

In the right triangle, tan(theta)=width/baseline, so width={{params.baseline}} tan({{params.angle}} degrees)={{correct_answers.width}} m.
