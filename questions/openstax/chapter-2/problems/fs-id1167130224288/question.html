Decide whether each equation permits cancellation to conclude A=B.

(a) A cross F = B cross F

Yes, always.No. A-B may be any vector parallel to F.

(b) A dot F = B dot F

No. A-B may be any vector perpendicular to F.Yes, always.

(c) fA=fB, where f is a scalar

Only if f is nonzero; if f=0 the equation gives no information about A and B.Yes, including when f=0.No, even when f is nonzero.

Dot and cross products discard some vector information, so they cannot generally be canceled. Multiplication by a nonzero scalar is invertible, but multiplication by zero is not.
