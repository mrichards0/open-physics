Vectors are A=({{params.ax}}, {{params.ay}}), B=({{params.bx}}, {{params.by}}), and F=({{params.fx}}, {{params.fy}}), in centimeters. Find G=A+2B-F.

Add components: G_x=A_x+2B_x-F_x={{correct_answers.gx}}, G_y=A_y+2B_y-F_y={{correct_answers.gy}}. Then |G|=sqrt(G_x^2+G_y^2)={{correct_answers.magnitude}} and theta=atan2(G_y,G_x)={{correct_answers.angle}} degrees counterclockwise from +x.
