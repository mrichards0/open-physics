Forces F1=({{params.f1x}}, {{params.f1y}}) N and F3=({{params.f3x}}, {{params.f3y}}) N act in a plane. Find the smaller angle between them.

Use cos(theta)=(F1 dot F3)/(|F1||F3|). The dot product and magnitudes give theta={{correct_answers.angle}} degrees.
