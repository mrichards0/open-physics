A vehicle moves with horizontal velocity components ({{params.vx}}, {{params.vy}}) m/s, where +x is east and +y is north. Find the x- and y-components of its direction unit vector and its direction angle north of east.

Divide each velocity component by |v|={{params.speed}} m/s. The unit vector is ({{correct_answers.ux}}, {{correct_answers.uy}}), whose magnitude is one, and its direction is {{correct_answers.angle}} degrees north of east.
