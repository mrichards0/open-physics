For A=A_x i + A_y j + A_z k, what is A dot i?

A_xA_yA_z|A|

What is A dot j?

A_yA_xA_z0 for every A

What is A dot k?

A_zA_x+A_yA_x|A|^2

The Cartesian basis is orthonormal: each basis vector has dot product one with itself and zero with the other two. A dot i therefore selects A_x, and similarly for j and k.
