Vectors A=({{params.ax}}, {{params.ay}}, 0), B=({{params.bx}}, {{params.by}}, 0), C=({{params.cx}}, {{params.cy}}, 0), and F=({{params.fx}}, {{params.fy}}, 0) lie in the xy-plane. Find the signed z-components of A cross B and C cross F.

For planar vectors, (U cross V)_z=U_xV_y-U_yV_x. Thus the results are {{correct_answers.abz}} and {{correct_answers.cfz}}; positive points out of the page and negative into it.
