|A|={{params.a}} cm, |B|={{params.b}} cm, and |A+B|={{params.r}} cm. Find the smaller angle between A and B.

From |A+B|^2=A^2+B^2+2AB cos(theta), solve cos(theta)=(R^2-A^2-B^2)/(2AB).
