Triangle vertices are O=(0,0), A=({{params.ax}},{{params.ay}}), and B=({{params.bx}},{{params.by}}). Let C=B-A be the side from A to B. A segment C' runs from the midpoint of OA to the midpoint of OB. Find C'_x, C'_y, and |C'|/|C|.

The two midpoints are A/2 and B/2. Their difference is B/2-A/2=(B-A)/2=C/2, so both components and the magnitude are halved.
