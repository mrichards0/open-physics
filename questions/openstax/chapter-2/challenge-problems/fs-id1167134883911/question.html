Coordinate system S' is obtained by rotating the axes of S counterclockwise by {{params.phi}} degrees. P=({{params.px}},{{params.py}}) and Q=({{params.qx}},{{params.qy}}) in S.

Using x'=x cos(phi)+y sin(phi), y'=-x sin(phi)+y cos(phi), find P' and Q'. Then calculate OP^2 in both systems and PQ^2 in both systems to verify invariance.

Apply the rotation matrix to each point. Expanding x'^2+y'^2 cancels the mixed terms and uses sin^2(phi)+cos^2(phi)=1. The same calculation applies to the coordinate differences P-Q.
