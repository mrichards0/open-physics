What best describes physics?

 The study of matter, energy, space, time, and their interactions to uncover the mechanisms of natural phenomena.A catalog of facts that never changes.The study only of machines and engineering devices.A branch of mathematics concerned only with abstract proofs.

Physics seeks general mechanisms governing natural phenomena, including interactions of matter and energy through space and time.
