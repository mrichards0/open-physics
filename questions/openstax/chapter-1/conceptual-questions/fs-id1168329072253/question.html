Two theories describe every available observation equally well and both use valid logic. Which conclusion is justified?

 Neither is currently more empirically valid; new discriminating evidence is needed.The older theory must be more valid.The mathematically longer theory must be more valid.Both theories must be false because they disagree.

Validity is decided by experiment. Preferences such as elegance cannot establish greater empirical validity when predictions are equally successful.
