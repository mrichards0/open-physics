What is a practical advantage of metric (SI) units?

 Prefixes scale units by powers of ten, making conversions and choice of an appropriate-sized unit systematic.Every physical quantity has exactly the same unit.Metric measurements have no uncertainty.Metric units eliminate the need for dimensional analysis.

SI prefixes express decimal powers, so scaling between units is systematic while retaining dimensional meaning.
