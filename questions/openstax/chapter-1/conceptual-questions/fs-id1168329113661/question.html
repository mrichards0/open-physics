Why is physics often described as a search for simplicity?

 A small set of broadly applicable laws can explain many apparently different phenomena.Physical systems are simple enough that approximations are never needed.Physics studies only situations containing one object.The simplest explanation is accepted even when experiments contradict it.

Physicists seek compact, testable principles that unify many observations; simplicity does not override experimental evidence.
