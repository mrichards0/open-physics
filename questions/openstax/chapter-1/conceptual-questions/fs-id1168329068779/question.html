What information is most useful when selecting equations to solve a physics problem?

 The known quantities, the requested unknowns, applicable physical principles, and assumptions or constraints.Only the numerical values, regardless of their units.The longest equation listed in the chapter.Only the final quantity requested; known information is irrelevant.

An equation is appropriate when it connects the knowns to the unknowns under conditions that match the governing principle and assumptions.
