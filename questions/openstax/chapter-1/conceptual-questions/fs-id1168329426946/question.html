What ultimately determines the scientific validity of a theory?

 Whether its testable predictions agree with reproducible observations and experiments.Whether its author is widely respected.Whether it is easy to understand.Whether it has remained unchanged for a long time.

A scientific theory must survive reproducible empirical tests; authority, popularity, and age are not decisive.
