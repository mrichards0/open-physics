After obtaining a numerical answer, what should you do?

 Check units and magnitude, test whether the result is physically reasonable, and assess its significance.Report it immediately without units.Round it to an integer so it appears simpler.Repeat the same arithmetic without considering the physical context.

A numerical result is not complete until its dimensions, scale, physical reasonableness, and implications have been assessed.
