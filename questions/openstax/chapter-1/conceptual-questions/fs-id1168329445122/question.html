Which statement correctly compares models with theories and laws?

 A model may be useful only within a stated range; theories and laws also have domains of applicability but must be consistently valid within those domains.Every model must describe all phenomena everywhere.A law may contradict experiments within its stated domain.A model is valid only if it contains no simplifying assumptions.

Scientific descriptions can have limited domains. A model can deliberately simplify, while any theory or law must agree with evidence throughout the domain where it claims to apply.
