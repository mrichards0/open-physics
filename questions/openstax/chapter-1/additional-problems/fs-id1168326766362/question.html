A runner covers {{params.distance_km}} km in {{params.hours}} h {{params.minutes}} min {{params.seconds}} s. Distance uncertainty is {{params.distance_uncertainty}} m and time uncertainty is {{params.time_uncertainty}} s. Find distance percent uncertainty, time percent uncertainty, average speed, and speed uncertainty. For the quotient, add fractional uncertainties.

Convert distance to meters and time to seconds. Percent uncertainties are 100 delta d/d and 100 delta t/t. Then v=d/t and delta v=v(delta d/d+delta t/t), giving {{correct_answers.speed}} plus or minus {{correct_answers.speed_uncertainty}} m/s.
