A piston has diameter {{params.diameter}} plus or minus {{params.ddiameter}} cm and travels {{params.travel}} plus or minus {{params.dtravel}} cm. Find the displaced volume and its conservative first-order uncertainty.

V=pi(d/2)^2 L={{correct_answers.volume}} cm^3. Since diameter is squared, delta V/V=2 delta d/d+delta L/L, giving {{correct_answers.uncertainty}} cm^3.
