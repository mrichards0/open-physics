A conversion is 1 lbm = {{params.kg_per_lbm}} plus or minus {{params.conversion_uncertainty}} kg. (a) Find its percent uncertainty. (b) What mass in lbm has conversion uncertainty {{params.target_kg}} kg?

The percentage is 100 delta c/c={{correct_answers.percent}}%. For M lbm, absolute conversion uncertainty is M delta c, so M={{params.target_kg}}/{{params.conversion_uncertainty}}={{correct_answers.mass_lbm}} lbm.
