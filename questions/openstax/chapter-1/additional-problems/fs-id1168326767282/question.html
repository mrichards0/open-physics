(a) Dimension and SI unit of s0?

L; mL/T; m/sL/T^2; m/s^2Dimensionless

(b) Dimension and SI unit of v0?

L/T; m/sL; mL/T^2; m/s^2LT; m s

(c) Dimension and SI unit of a0?

L/T^2; m/s^2L/T; m/sL/T^3; m/s^3L T^2; m s^2

(d) Dimension and SI unit of j0?

L/T^3; m/s^3L/T^2; m/s^2L/T^4; m/s^4L T^3; m s^3

(e) Dimension and SI unit of S0?

L/T^4; m/s^4L/T^3; m/s^3L/T^5; m/s^5L^4/T; m^4/s

(f) Dimension and SI unit of c?

L/T^5; m/s^5L/T^4; m/s^4L/T^6; m/s^6L^5/T; m^5/s

Each term must be a length. Therefore the coefficient multiplying t^n has dimension L/T^n and SI unit m/s^n.
