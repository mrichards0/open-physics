(a) In y=mt+b, with [y]=L and [t]=T, what are the dimension and SI unit of m?

L/T; meters per secondL; metersLT; meter-secondsL/T^2; meters per second squared

(b) What are the dimension and SI unit of b?

L; metersL/T; meters per secondT; secondsDimensionless; no unit

Every term must have dimension L. Since mt has dimension L, m must have L/T; the additive constant b must itself have L.
