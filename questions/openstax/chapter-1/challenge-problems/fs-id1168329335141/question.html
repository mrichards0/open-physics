Assume blast radius r=k E^a rho^b t^c, with [E]=ML^2T^-2 and [rho]=ML^-3.

(a) Find a, b, and c. (b) For k={{params.k}}, r={{params.radius}} m at t={{params.time_ms}} ms in air density {{params.density}} kg/m^3, find E. (c) Convert E to kilotons TNT using 1 kt TNT = 4.2 times 10^12 J.

Matching M, L, and T exponents gives a=1/5, b=-1/5, c=2/5. Raising r=k(E/rho)^(1/5)t^(2/5) to the fifth power gives E=rho r^5/(k^5 t^2). The results are {{correct_answers.energy}} J and {{correct_answers.kilotons}} kt TNT.
