Is $v=at$ dimensionally consistent, where $v$ is velocity, $a$ is acceleration, and $t$ is time?

 Yes; $[a][t]=(L/T^2)(T)=L/T=[v]$.No; acceleration and time cannot be multiplied.No; $[a][t]=L/T^3$.Yes; both sides have dimensions $L/T^2$.

Multiplying acceleration by time gives $(L/T^2)T=L/T$, exactly the dimension of velocity.
