Which expression has the dimensions of the volume of a sphere of radius $r$?

 $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$4\pi r^2$$\pi r^2$$\frac{4}{3}\pi r^2$

Volume must have dimension $L^3$. Only $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$ contains the third power of a length; $4\pi r^2$ is the sphere's surface area.
