An object's diameter is on the order of $10^{{params.diameter_exp}}\ \mathrm{m}$. Estimate the order of magnitude of its surface area in $\mathrm{km^2}$ by squaring this length scale.

Squaring gives $10^{2({{params.diameter_exp}})}\ \mathrm{m^2}$. Since $1\ \mathrm{km^2}=10^6\ \mathrm{m^2}$, the result is $10^{{params.area_exp}}={{correct_answers.area}}\ \mathrm{km^2}$.
