A force-analysis program receives a force of ${{params.force_lbf}}\ \mathrm{lbf}$ but mistakenly displays the same numerical value, ${{params.force_lbf}}$, with units of newtons. Use $1\ \mathrm{lbf}=4.45\ \mathrm{N}$.

(a) What force should the program display in newtons?

(b) How does the program's incorrect numerical output compare with the correct value?

 It is too small by a factor of 4.45. It is too large by a factor of 4.45. It is too small by a factor of $1/4.45$. It is correct because the numerical value does not change.

Multiply pound-force by the conversion factor: ${{params.force_lbf}}\ \mathrm{lbf}(4.45\ \mathrm{N}/\mathrm{lbf}) ={{correct_answers.force_newtons}}\ \mathrm{N}$. The program displayed only ${{params.force_lbf}}\ \mathrm{N}$, so its output was too small by the factor $4.45$.
