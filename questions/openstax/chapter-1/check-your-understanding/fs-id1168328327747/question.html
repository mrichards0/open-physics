A signal travels ${{params.distance_pm}}\ \mathrm{Pm}$ in ${{params.time_million_s}}\times10^6\ \mathrm{s}$. Recall that $1\ \mathrm{Pm}=10^{15}\ \mathrm{m}$.

Calculate its average speed in meters per second.

Convert the distance and divide by the elapsed time: \[ v=\frac{d}{\Delta t} =\frac{({{params.distance_pm}}\ \mathrm{Pm})(10^{15}\ \mathrm{m/Pm})} {({{params.time_million_s}}\times10^6\ \mathrm{s})} ={{correct_answers.speed}}\ \mathrm{m/s}. \]
