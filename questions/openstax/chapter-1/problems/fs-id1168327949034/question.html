The quotient 4000/400 is reported as 10.0, and the reported three significant figures are correct. What follows about the operands?

Neither operand has fewer than three significant figures, and at least one has exactly three; clearer notation is needed to show which trailing zeros are significant.Both operands must have exactly one significant figure.Both operands must have exactly four significant figures.The quotient rule depends only on decimal places, so nothing follows.

For multiplication or division, the result has as many significant figures as the least precise operand. A three-significant-figure result requires a minimum of three among the operands; ordinary trailing-zero notation is ambiguous.
