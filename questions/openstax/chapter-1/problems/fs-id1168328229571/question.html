(a) Two bags weigh 13.5 lb each and a third weighs 10.2 lb. Which properly rounded total follows the addition rule?

(b) A 55 kg wagon accelerates at 0.0255 m/s^2. Which force is properly rounded using F=ma?

1.4 N1.4025 N1.40 N0.46 N

Addition is rounded to the least precise decimal place: 13.5+13.5+10.2=37.2 lb. Multiplication is limited by the factor with fewer significant figures: 55(0.0255)=1.4025 N, reported as 1.4 N.
