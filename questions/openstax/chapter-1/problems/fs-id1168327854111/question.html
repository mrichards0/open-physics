A posted speed limit is ${{params.kmh}}\ \mathrm{km/h}$. Convert it to meters per second and miles per hour.

Use $1\ \mathrm{km}=1000\ \mathrm{m}$ and $1\ \mathrm{h}=3600\ \mathrm{s}$, giving ${{correct_answers.mps}}\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Multiplying km/h by $0.621371$ gives ${{correct_answers.mph}}\ \mathrm{mi/h}$.
