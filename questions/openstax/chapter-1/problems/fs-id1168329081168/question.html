A moon has mass {{params.mass_coeff}} times 10^22 kg, radius {{params.radius_km}} km, and angular diameter {{params.angle_deg}} degrees.

(a) Estimate its average density. (b) Find its physical diameter. (c) Using the small-angle relation theta=D/d, estimate its distance.

Use rho=M/(4 pi R^3/3), D=2R, and d=D/theta with theta converted to radians. Results: {{correct_answers.density}} kg/m^3, {{correct_answers.diameter}} km, and {{correct_answers.distance}} km.
