A tectonic plate moves at ${{params.cm_per_year}}\ \mathrm{cm/yr}$. Use $365.25$ days per year.

(a) How far does it move in one second? (b) Express its speed in kilometers per million years.

Dividing the annual motion by the seconds in a year gives {{correct_answers.meters_per_second}} m in one second. Over $10^6$ years, converting centimeters to kilometers gives {{correct_answers.km_per_myr}} km/Myr.
