A speed is ${{params.mps}}\ \mathrm{m/s}$. Convert it to kilometers per hour and miles per hour.

Multiplying by $3600\ \mathrm{s/h}$ and dividing by the length conversion gives ${{correct_answers.kmh}}\ \mathrm{km/h}$ and ${{correct_answers.mph}}\ \mathrm{mi/h}$.
