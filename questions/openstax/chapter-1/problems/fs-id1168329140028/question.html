A supercomputer sustains {{params.coefficient}} times 10^{{params.exponent}} operations per second. How many operations can it perform during a {{params.years}}-year human lifetime?

Multiply rate by {{params.years}} times 365.25 times 86400 seconds, giving {{correct_answers.operations}} operations.
