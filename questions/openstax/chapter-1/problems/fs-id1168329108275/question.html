A quantity is {{params.coefficient}} times 10^{{params.exponent}} {{params.unit}}. Give the integer exponent n for its nearest order of magnitude 10^n.

The boundary between 10^n and 10^(n+1) is sqrt(10) times 10^n. Since the coefficient is {{params.coefficient}}, the nearest power is 10^{{correct_answers.order_exponent}}.
