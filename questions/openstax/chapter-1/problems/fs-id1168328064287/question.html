A substance has density {{params.g_cm3}} g/cm^3. Convert it to kg/m^3.

Because 1 g = 10^-3 kg and 1 cm^3 = 10^-6 m^3, 1 g/cm^3 = 1000 kg/m^3. The result is {{correct_answers.kg_m3}} kg/m^3.
