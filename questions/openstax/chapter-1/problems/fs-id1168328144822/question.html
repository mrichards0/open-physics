(a) Given [rho]=M/L^3 and [V]=L^3, what is the dimension of the integral of rho dV?

MM/L^3ML^3M/T

(b) What is the dimension of dV/dt?

L^3/TL^2/TL^3 TL/T

(c) What is the dimension of rho(dV/dt)?

M/TMM/L^3TML^3/T

The volume element supplies L^3, canceling L^-3 in density. A volume rate is L^3/T, so density times volume rate is mass per time.
