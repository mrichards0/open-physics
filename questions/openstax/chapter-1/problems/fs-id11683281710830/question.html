A person counts {{params.beats}} plus or minus {{params.beat_uncertainty}} beats during {{params.time}} plus or minus {{params.time_uncertainty}} s. Find (a) heart rate and (b) its uncertainty in beats/min. For a quotient, use the conservative first-order rule that fractional uncertainties add.

R=60N/t={{correct_answers.rate}} beats/min. The fractional uncertainty is delta N/N + delta t/t, so delta R=R({{params.beat_uncertainty}}/{{params.beats}}+{{params.time_uncertainty}}/{{params.time}})={{correct_answers.rate_uncertainty}} beats/min.
