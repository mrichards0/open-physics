(a) Is v^2 = 2as dimensionally consistent?

Yes: both sides have dimension L^2/T^2.No: the right side has L/T^2.No: the left side has L/T.

(b) Is s = vt^2 + 0.5at^2 dimensionally consistent?

No: vt^2 has dimension LT while at^2 has L, so the terms cannot be added.Yes: every term has dimension L.No: both terms on the right have dimension LT.

(c) Is v = s/t dimensionally consistent?

Yes: both sides have dimension L/T.No: s/t has dimension LT.No: s/t has dimension L/T^2.

(d) Is a = v/t dimensionally consistent?

Yes: both sides have dimension L/T^2.No: v/t has dimension L/T.No: v/t has dimension L/T^3.

Substitute [s]=L, [v]=L/T, [a]=L/T^2, and [t]=T, and require all added terms to match.
