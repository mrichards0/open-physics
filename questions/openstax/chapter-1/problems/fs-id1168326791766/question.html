Assume a person could perform {{params.human_rate}} arithmetic operations each second continuously for {{params.years}} years. A supercomputer performs {{params.super_coeff}} times 10^{{params.super_exp}} operations per second.

(a) Estimate the person's lifetime operations. (b) How many seconds would the supercomputer need for the same count?

Multiply the human rate by {{params.years}}(365.25)(86400) seconds to obtain {{correct_answers.human_ops}} operations. Dividing by the computer rate gives {{correct_answers.computer_seconds}} s.
