A supercomputer sustains {{params.coefficient}} times 10^{{params.exponent}} floating-point operations per second. Approximately how many operations does it perform in one year? Use 365.25 days.

Multiply the rate by 365.25 times 86400 seconds/year, giving {{correct_answers.operations}} operations.
