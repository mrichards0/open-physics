A person's height is ${{params.feet}}\ \mathrm{ft}\ {{params.inches}}\ \mathrm{in}$. Convert the total height to meters.

The total is $12({{params.feet}})+{{params.inches}}={{params.total_inches}}$ inches. Multiplying by $0.0254\ \mathrm{m/in}$ gives {{correct_answers.meters}} m.
