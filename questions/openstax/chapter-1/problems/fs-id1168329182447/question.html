A bacterium has mass {{params.b_coeff}} times 10^{{params.b_exp}} kg. Assume its average atom has mass {{params.multiple}} times the proton mass, where m_p={{params.p_coeff}} times 10^-27 kg. Estimate its number of atoms.

Divide bacterial mass by average atomic mass: N=m_b/({{params.multiple}}m_p)={{correct_answers.atoms}}.
