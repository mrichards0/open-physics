Model a standing person as a closed cylinder of height {{params.height}} m and radius {{params.radius}} m. Estimate the total surface area, including both circular ends.

For a closed cylinder, A=2 pi r h + 2 pi r^2. Substitution gives {{correct_answers.area}} m^2.
