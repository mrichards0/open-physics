(a) What is the dimension of arc length s?

LL^2Dimensionless1/L

(b) What is the dimension of radius r?

LL^2Dimensionless1/L

(c) In s=r theta, what is the dimension of theta measured in radians?

DimensionlessL1/LT

Since s and r both have dimension L, theta=s/r is a ratio of lengths and is dimensionless.
