A volume is ${{params.coefficient}}\times10^{{params.exponent}}\ \mathrm{m^3}$. Convert it to each requested unit.

Cubic conversion factors must be cubed: $1\ \mathrm{km^3}=10^9\ \mathrm{m^3}$, $1\ \mathrm{mi^3}=(1609.344)^3\ \mathrm{m^3}$, and $1\ \mathrm{m^3}=10^6\ \mathrm{cm^3}$. The answers are {{correct_answers.km3}}, {{correct_answers.mi3}}, and {{correct_answers.cm3}}, respectively.
