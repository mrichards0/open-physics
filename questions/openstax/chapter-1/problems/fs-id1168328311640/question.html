A material has density {{params.coefficient}} times 10^{{params.exponent}} kg/m^3. Convert it to Mg per microliter.

Use 1 Mg = 10^3 kg and 1 m^3 = 10^9 microliters. Multiplying kg/m^3 by 10^-12 gives {{correct_answers.density}} Mg/microliter.
