A nerve impulse occupies the nerve for {{params.duration_ms}} ms, and one impulse must end before another begins. What is the maximum firing rate?

The maximum rate is the reciprocal duration: f=1/({{params.duration_ms}} times 10^-3 s)={{correct_answers.rate}} impulses/s.
