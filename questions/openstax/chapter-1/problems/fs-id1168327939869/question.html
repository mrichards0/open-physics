A mountain is ${{params.feet}}\ \mathrm{ft}$ high. Convert its height to kilometers using $1\ \mathrm{m}=3.281\ \mathrm{ft}$.

Divide by $3.281\ \mathrm{ft/m}$ and then by $1000\ \mathrm{m/km}$: $h={{correct_answers.kilometers}}\ \mathrm{km}$.
