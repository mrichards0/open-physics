A star has mass {{params.mass_coeff}} times 10^30 kg, average density {{params.density}} kg/m^3, and angular diameter {{params.angle_deg}} degrees.

(a) Estimate its physical diameter. (b) Estimate its distance using theta=D/d.

From M=rho(4 pi R^3/3), solve R=[3M/(4 pi rho)]^(1/3), then D=2R. The small-angle estimate is d=D/theta. Results are {{correct_answers.diameter_km}} km and {{correct_answers.distance_km}} km.
