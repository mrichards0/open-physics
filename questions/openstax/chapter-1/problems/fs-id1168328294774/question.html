A planet follows a circular orbit of radius {{params.radius_1e11}} times 10^11 m with period {{params.period_days}} days. Find its speed.

For a circular orbit, v = 2 pi r/T. Converting days to seconds gives {{correct_answers.mps}} m/s; converting m/s to mi/h gives {{correct_answers.mph}} mi/h.
