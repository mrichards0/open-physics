A container holds {{params.fl_oz}} fluid ounces. Using 1 fl oz approximately 30 mL, express its volume in m^3.

Multiply by 30 mL/fl oz and 10^-6 m^3/mL. The volume is {{correct_answers.m3}} m^3.
