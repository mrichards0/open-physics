Take a generation to be one-third of a {{params.lifetime}}-year lifetime. Approximately how many generations fit in {{params.elapsed_years}} years?

Generation length is {{params.lifetime}}/3 years, so N={{params.elapsed_years}}/({{params.lifetime}}/3)={{correct_answers.generations}}.
