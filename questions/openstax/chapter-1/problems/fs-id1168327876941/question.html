(a) If v=ds/dt, what is [v]?

L/TLTL/T^2T/L

(b) If a=dv/dt, what is [a]?

L/T^2L/TL/T^3LT^2

(c) What is the dimension of the integral of v dt?

LL/TLTL/T^2

(d) What is the dimension of the integral of a dt?

L/TLL/T^2LT

(e) What is the dimension of da/dt?

L/T^3L/T^2L/TLT^3

A time derivative divides dimensions by T; integration with respect to time multiplies them by T.
