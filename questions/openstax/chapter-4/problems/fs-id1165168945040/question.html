A runner completes a {{params.distance}}-m race in {{params.time}} s at constant speed. Part of the track is a circular arc with radius {{params.radius}} m. Find the runner's speed and centripetal acceleration on the arc.

Constant speed is total race distance divided by total time. On the curved portion, a_c=v^2/r.
