A ship heads due north at {{params.ship}} m/s relative to the water. The current is {{params.current}} m/s at {{params.angle}} degrees north of east. Use +x east and +y north. Find the ship's Earth-frame velocity, speed, and direction east of north.

Add the ship-through-water and current vectors. The current components are u cos(alpha) east and u sin(alpha) north. Use atan2(v_x,v_y) for the angle east of north.
