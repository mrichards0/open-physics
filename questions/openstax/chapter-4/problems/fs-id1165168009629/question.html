A motorcycle leaves a ramp at {{params.speed}} m/s and lands at the same elevation {{params.range}} m away. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2 and neglect air resistance. Find both possible launch angles.

For equal launch and landing elevations, R=v0^2 sin(2 theta)/g. Inverse sine gives 2 theta for the lower trajectory. The complementary value of 2 theta gives the higher trajectory, so the two launch angles sum to 90 degrees.
