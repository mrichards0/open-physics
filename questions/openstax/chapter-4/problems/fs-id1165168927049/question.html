A vehicle travels in a circular path just above a spherical world's surface. The world has radius {{params.radius}} km and surface gravitational acceleration {{params.g}} m/s^2. What speed makes the vehicle's centripetal acceleration equal g?

Set v^2/R=g, convert the radius to meters, and solve v=sqrt(gR). Divide by 1000 to express the result in km/s.
