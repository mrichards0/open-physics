A robot launches at {{params.kmh}} km/h and {{params.angle}} degrees. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. (a) Find maximum height. (b) What smaller launch angle reaches target height {{params.target}} m at the same speed?

Convert speed to m/s. Height is v0^2 sin^2(theta)/(2g). For the target, sin(theta)=sqrt(2gh)/v0; inverse sine gives the smaller angle.
