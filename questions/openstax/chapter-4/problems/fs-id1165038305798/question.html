A jet reaches {{params.v0}} m/s from rest over a {{params.L}} m carrier deck. After leaving, it has constant acceleration magnitude {{params.A}} m/s^2 at {{params.angle}} degrees above horizontal. At t={{params.t}} s after deck exit, find deck acceleration, airborne acceleration components, altitude, velocity components/speed, and horizontal distance.

On deck, a=v0^2/(2L). Airborne acceleration resolves into A(cos theta,sin theta). With initial velocity (v0,0), use constant-acceleration component equations.
