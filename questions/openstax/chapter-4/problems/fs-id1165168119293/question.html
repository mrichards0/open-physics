A ball is kicked from ground {{params.x}} m from a crossbar {{params.h}} m high at {{params.angle}} degrees. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find required initial speed and time to the crossbar.

Substitute t=x/(v0cos theta) into h=x tan theta-gx^2/[2v0^2cos^2 theta], solve v0, then find t.
