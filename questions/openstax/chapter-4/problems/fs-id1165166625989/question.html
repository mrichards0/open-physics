A balloon is {{params.h}} m high and descending at {{params.down}} m/s when a passenger throws a ball horizontally at {{params.horizontal}} m/s relative to the balloon. The balloon has no horizontal motion. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find ball impact time, horizontal landing distance from release, and signed vertical impact velocity.

The ball inherits downward initial velocity -u. Solve 0=h-ut-(1/2)gt^2 for positive time. Horizontal speed is constant and v_y=-u-gt.
