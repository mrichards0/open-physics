A passer throws a ball at {{params.ball_speed}} m/s. A receiver runs directly away at {{params.run_speed}} m/s, starting {{params.start}} m ahead. When the receiver is {{params.finish}} m ahead, the ball must be back at its launch height. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

(a) Find the launch angle required for that flight time. (b) Find the ball's horizontal position when it returns to launch height, then decide whether the receiver can catch it at the stated location.

Yes; the ball and receiver are at the same horizontal position.No; their horizontal positions differ at that time.

The receiver takes t=(x_f-x_i)/u. A projectile returning to launch height has t=2v0 sin(theta)/g, which determines theta. Its horizontal coordinate at that instant is x=v0 cos(theta)t; compare this with the receiver's stated position.
