For t>0, r(t)=({{params.A}}t^2)i+({{params.B}}t^3)j+({{params.C}}t^-2)k meters.

(a) Express velocity as (v1 t,v2 t^2,v3 t^-3) and acceleration as (a0,a1 t,a2 t^-4). (b) Find velocity at t={{params.te}}, speeds at t={{params.ts1}} and {{params.ts2}}, and average velocity components from t={{params.ta}} to {{params.tb}}.

Differentiate each power twice. Evaluate velocity components directly; speed is their magnitude. Average velocity is [r(tb)-r(ta)]/(tb-ta).
