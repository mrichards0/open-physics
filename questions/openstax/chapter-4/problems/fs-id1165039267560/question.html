An airplane's airspeed is {{params.plane}} km/h. Wind blows due east at {{params.wind}} km/h. Find the heading angle west of north required for a due-north ground track, the resulting northward ground speed, and the time to travel {{params.distance}} km north.

The plane's westward air-velocity component must equal the eastward wind: sin(theta)=u/v. Its north component, and therefore ground speed, is sqrt(v^2-u^2). Time is distance divided by ground speed.
