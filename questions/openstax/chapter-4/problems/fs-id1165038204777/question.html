A particle's rectangular coordinates are ({{params.x}},{{params.y}},{{params.z}}) {{params.unit}}. Give the three scalar components of its position vector.

In a Cartesian basis, the position vector is r=x i+y j+z k; the listed coordinates are its scalar components.
