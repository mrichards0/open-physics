A particle has constant acceleration. At t=0, v=({{params.v0x}},{{params.v0y}}) m/s; at t={{params.T}} s, v=({{params.vTx}},{{params.vTy}}) m/s. It starts at the origin.

Find acceleration components. Write x=c1t+c2t^2, y=d1t+d2t^2 and v_x=e0+e1t, v_y=f0+f1t; find all coefficients.

Constant acceleration is (vT-v0)/T. Velocity is v0+at, and position from the origin is v0t+(1/2)at^2.
