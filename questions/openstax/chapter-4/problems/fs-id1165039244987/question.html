A river flows east at {{params.current}} m/s. A boat moves at {{params.boat}} m/s relative to the water, heading {{params.angle}} degrees north of west. The north-south river width is {{params.width}} m. Use +x east and +y north. Find the boat's Earth-frame velocity components and speed, then its crossing time and eastward displacement during the crossing.

The boat-through-water components are (-v cos(theta), v sin(theta)); add the current to the x component. Crossing time is width/v_y, and signed eastward displacement is v_xt.
