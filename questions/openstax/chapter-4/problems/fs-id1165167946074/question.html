A projectile launches at {{params.angle}} degrees and lands at launch height after {{params.T}} s. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find initial speed, maximum altitude above launch, range, and displacement components/magnitude at t={{params.te}} s.

Same-height flight time gives v0 sin(theta)=gT/2. Then use standard component equations; maximum height is v_y0^2/(2g).
