A ball launches from {{params.y0}} m at {{params.angle}} degrees and must pass through a basket {{params.x}} m away at height {{params.yf}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find required initial speed and travel time to the basket.

Substitute t=x/(v0 cos theta) into Delta y=v0 sin(theta)t-(1/2)gt^2. Solving gives v0^2=gx^2/[2cos^2(theta)(x tan(theta)-Delta y)].
