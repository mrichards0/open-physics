A fan rotates steadily at {{params.rpm}} rev/min. A point on a blade is {{params.radius_cm}} cm from the axis. Find its angular speed, tangential speed, and centripetal acceleration.

Convert rev/min to rev/s, then omega=2 pi f. With radius in meters, v=omega r and a_c=omega^2r.
