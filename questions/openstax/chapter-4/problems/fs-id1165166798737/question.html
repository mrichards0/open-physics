A ball launches at {{params.v}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees and is caught at launch height. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find range and flight time.

For equal heights, R=v0^2 sin(2theta)/g and T=2v0 sin(theta)/g.
