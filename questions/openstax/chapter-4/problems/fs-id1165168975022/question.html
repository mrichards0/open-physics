Riders move in a horizontal circle of radius {{params.radius}} m. At what rotation rate will their centripetal acceleration equal {{params.multiple}}g? Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Set v^2/r=kg and solve v=sqrt(kgr). Then f=v/(2 pi r) in rev/s and multiply by 60 for rev/min.
