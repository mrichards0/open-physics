A ball is released horizontally at {{params.v}} m/s from {{params.d}} m away. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find travel time and vertical drop.

Horizontal motion gives t=d/v. The vertical initial velocity is zero, so drop=(1/2)gt^2.
