A marble rolls horizontally from a {{params.h}} m table and lands {{params.range}} m away. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find flight time, launch speed, vertical impact component, and impact speed.

t=sqrt(2h/g), v_x=range/t, and v_y=-gt. Impact speed is hypot(v_x,v_y).
