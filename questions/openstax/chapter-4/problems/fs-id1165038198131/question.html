A particle has r(t)=({{params.a}}t^2)i+({{params.y}})j+({{params.b}}t^3)k meters. Find velocity components at t=0 and t={{params.t}} s, and average velocity components over that interval.

Differentiate to get v=(2at,0,3bt^2). Average velocity is [r(t)-r(0)]/t=(at,0,bt^2).
