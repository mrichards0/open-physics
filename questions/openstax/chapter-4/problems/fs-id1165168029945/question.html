From a summit {{params.h}} km above the surrounding plain, a projectile must be fired horizontally to land beyond a volcano radius of {{params.r}} km. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find minimum horizontal speed, flight time, and signed vertical impact velocity.

Convert distances to meters. Fall time is sqrt(2h/g); minimum speed is radius divided by that time, and v_y=-gt.
