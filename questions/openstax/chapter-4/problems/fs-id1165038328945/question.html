A particle has r(t)=({{params.p}}t^2)i+({{params.y}})j+({{params.q}}t)k meters.

(a) Express velocity as (c_x t,c_y,c_z) and acceleration as (a_x,a_y,a_z). (b) Give velocity and acceleration components at t=0.

Differentiate r: v=(2pt,0,q). Differentiate again: a=(2p,0,0). At t=0 the x velocity is zero while z velocity remains q.
