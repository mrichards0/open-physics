A golfer is {{params.green}} m horizontally from a green {{params.drop}} m below launch. The ball leaves at {{params.v0}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees above horizontal. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find landing time, horizontal landing position, signed miss (landing x minus green x), and miss distance.

Solve -drop=v_y0t-(1/2)gt^2 for positive time, then x=v_x0t. Signed miss distinguishes long from short; its magnitude is closeness.
