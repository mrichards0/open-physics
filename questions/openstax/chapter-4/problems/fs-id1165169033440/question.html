A point on a rotating hand has radial acceleration {{params.accel}} cm/s^2. The hand completes one revolution every {{params.period}} s. How far is the point from the rotation axis?

The angular speed is omega=2 pi/T and radial acceleration is a_r=omega^2r. Thus r=a_r/omega^2; convert centimeters to meters for the second response.
