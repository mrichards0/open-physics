Take +x straight down the rink and +y across it. A puck first travels {{params.d1}} m at {{params.angle}} degrees toward +y from +x, then {{params.d2}} m directly across toward -y. Find final displacement components, magnitude, and direction counterclockwise from +x.

The first vector is (d1 cos theta,d1 sin theta); the second is (0,-d2). Add, then use hypot and normalized atan2.
