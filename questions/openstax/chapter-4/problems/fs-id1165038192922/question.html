A cyclist rides {{params.east}} km east, then {{params.middle}} km at {{params.angle}} degrees west of north, then {{params.west}} km west. Find final displacement components, magnitude, and direction west of north (signed; negative means east of north).

The middle leg is (-L sin theta,L cos theta). Add the east/west legs. Magnitude is hypot(x,y), and direction west of north is atan2(-x,y).
