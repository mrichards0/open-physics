Rocks launch at {{params.angle}} degrees and land {{params.x}} m horizontally away and {{params.drop}} m below launch. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find initial speed and flight time.

Use -drop=x tan(theta)-gx^2/[2v0^2cos^2(theta)] to solve v0, then t=x/[v0cos(theta)].
