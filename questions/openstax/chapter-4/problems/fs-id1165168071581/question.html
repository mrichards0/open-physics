A projectile launches from the origin at {{params.v0}} m/s and {{params.launch}} degrees. A hillside is y=tan({{params.slope}} degrees)x-{{params.intercept}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find impact coordinates and distance along the hill measured from its base (where y=0).

Set projectile y=x tan(alpha)-gx^2/[2v0^2cos^2(alpha)] equal to the hill line and solve the quadratic. Use the positive intersection beyond the hill base; then compute distance between that point and the base.
