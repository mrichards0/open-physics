A rock launches from a {{params.h}} m cliff at {{params.v0}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees above horizontal. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find rise above cliff, horizontal position at apex, impact time, and range. Also find x/y relative to the cliff edge at t={{params.t1}}, {{params.t2}}, and {{params.t3}} s.

Resolve initial velocity. Apex time is vy0/g; rise=vy0^2/(2g). Solve -h=vy0t-(1/2)gt^2 for impact. At any time, x=vx0t and y=vy0t-(1/2)gt^2.
