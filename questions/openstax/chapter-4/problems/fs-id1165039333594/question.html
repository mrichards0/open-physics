A cyclist travels southeast at {{params.bike}} km/h. Relative to the cyclist, the wind blows toward the northeast at {{params.apparent}} km/h. Use +x east and +y north. Find the true wind's Earth-frame components, speed, and direction north of east.

v_wind/Earth=v_wind/cyclist+v_cyclist/Earth. Southeast has equal positive-east and negative-north components; northeast has equal positive components. Add components, then use magnitude and atan2(v_y,v_x).
