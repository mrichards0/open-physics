An object spins at {{params.rate}} rev/s. A point {{params.radius_cm}} cm from its rotation axis follows a circular path. Find that point's angular speed and centripetal acceleration.

Convert radius to meters and use omega=2 pi f. The centripetal acceleration is a_c=omega^2 r.
