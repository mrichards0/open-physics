An athlete's range is {{params.earth}} m on Earth. With identical launch speed/angle, another world's gravity is 1/{{params.factor}} of Earth's. Find the new range.

For fixed launch speed and angle, R=v0^2 sin(2theta)/g, so range is inversely proportional to gravity.
