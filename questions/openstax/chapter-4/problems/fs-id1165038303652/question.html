A particle follows r(t)=R cos(omega t)i+R sin(omega t)j+c t k. Express velocity as (-K sin(omega t),K cos(omega t),c) and acceleration as (-L cos(omega t),-L sin(omega t),0). For R={{params.R}}, omega={{params.omega}}, c={{params.c}}, find K, L, and all velocity/acceleration components at t={{params.t}} s.

Differentiate using the chain rule: K=R omega and L=R omega^2. Substitute the generated time into the sine and cosine components.
