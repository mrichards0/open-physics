A particle starts from rest at the origin with constant acceleration ({{params.ax}},{{params.ay}}) m/s^2.

(a) Write x=cx t^2, y=cy t^2, vx=dx t, vy=dy t. Find the four coefficients. (b) Write its path y=mx and find m.

Integrating constant acceleration from zero velocity gives v=at, and integrating again from the origin gives r=(1/2)at^2. Eliminating t^2 gives y=(ay/ax)x.
