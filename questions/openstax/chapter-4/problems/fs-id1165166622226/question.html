An airplane moves horizontally at {{params.plane}} m/s at altitude {{params.h}} m and fires a projectile forward at {{params.relative}} m/s relative to the plane. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find ground-frame launch speed, fall time, horizontal range, signed vertical impact velocity, and impact speed.

Add the collinear airplane and relative projectile velocities. Vertical fall gives t=sqrt(2h/g); horizontal velocity remains constant and v_y=-gt.
