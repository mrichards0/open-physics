A motorcycle moves at {{params.vx}} m/s. Its rider throws a can upward at {{params.vy}} m/s relative to himself. A roadside observer uses the release point as origin. Write trajectory y=c1 x+c2 x^2 and find its maximum height and horizontal apex position. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Ground-frame components are (vx,vy). With t=x/vx, y=(vy/vx)x-[g/(2vx^2)]x^2. The apex occurs at t=vy/g.
