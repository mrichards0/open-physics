A particle moves from r1=({{params.x1}},{{params.y1}},{{params.z1}}) cm to r2=({{params.x2}},{{params.y2}},{{params.z2}}) cm. Find displacement components and magnitude.

Displacement is r2-r1 component by component. Its magnitude is sqrt(dx^2+dy^2+dz^2).
