A boat moves at {{params.boat}} km/h relative to still water. A river flows east at {{params.current}} km/h. A destination is {{params.along}} km along the river, and the river is {{params.width}} km wide.

(a) Find downstream and upstream travel times. (b) To cross straight north, find the heading angle west of north, Earth-frame crossing speed, and crossing time. (c) If instead aimed due north, find crossing time and downstream drift.

Alongstream ground speeds are b+u and b-u. For no drift, the boat's west component must cancel the current: sin(theta)=u/b, leaving north speed sqrt(b^2-u^2). If aimed north, north speed remains b while the current produces eastward drift ut.
