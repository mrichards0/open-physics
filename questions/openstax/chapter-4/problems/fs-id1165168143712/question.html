A bullet is fired horizontally at {{params.v0}} m/s from height {{params.h}} m. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find flight time and horizontal range.

Vertical fall gives t=sqrt(2h/g); horizontal speed is constant, so range=v0t.
