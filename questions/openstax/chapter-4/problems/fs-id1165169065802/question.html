A planet follows an approximately circular orbit with radius {{params.radius_million}} million km and period {{params.days}} days. Find its centripetal acceleration toward its star.

Convert orbital radius to meters and period to seconds. For uniform circular motion, a_c=4 pi^2 r/T^2.
