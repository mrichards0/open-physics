A particle moves at constant speed {{params.speed}} m/s in a circle of radius {{params.radius}} m. Find the magnitude of its acceleration and state its direction.

Radially inward, toward the centerRadially outward, away from the centerTangent to the path in the direction of motionZero, because the speed is constant

Uniform circular motion has centripetal acceleration a_c=v^2/r. Although speed is constant, velocity changes direction, and its acceleration points toward the center.
