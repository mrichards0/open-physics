A boat leaves the origin with initial wind-driven velocity ({{params.vx0}},{{params.vy0}}) m/s and constant acceleration ({{params.ax}},0) m/s^2. Find velocity and position components at t={{params.t}} s and identify the trajectory shape.

A curved path: y grows linearly in time while x has both linear and quadratic terms.A straight line at constant slope because acceleration is zero.A circle centered at the dock.

v=(vx0+ax t,vy0), and r=(vx0t+(1/2)ax t^2,vy0t). Eliminating time generally produces a curved path.
