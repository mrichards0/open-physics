A bird flies at {{params.air}} m/s in still air. Flying straight into a headwind, it covers {{params.distance}} km in {{params.minutes}} min. Find the wind speed and the time required to fly the same distance back with the wind.

Convert distance and time to SI units. Upwind, v_ground=v_air-v_wind. On return, the speeds add; divide the distance by that ground speed and convert seconds to minutes.
