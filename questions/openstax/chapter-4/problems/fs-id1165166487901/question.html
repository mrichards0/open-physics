A ball launches at {{params.v}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees on level ground. Find its range where gravity is {{params.g1}} m/s^2 and where gravity is {{params.g2}} m/s^2, plus the ratio R2/R1.

Same-height range is R=v0^2 sin(2theta)/g. With speed and angle fixed, range is inversely proportional to g.
