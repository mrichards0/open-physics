A thrower's maximum level-ground horizontal range is {{params.range}} m, with the same launch speed available at all angles. How high would the ball rise if thrown straight upward?

Maximum range at 45 degrees is R=v0^2/g. A vertical throw rises h=v0^2/(2g)=R/2.
