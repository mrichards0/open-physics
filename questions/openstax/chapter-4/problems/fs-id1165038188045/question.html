Take +x along the midfield line and +y downfield. An athlete runs {{params.d1}} m at {{params.angle}} degrees from +x in {{params.t1}} s, then {{params.d2}} m along +y in {{params.t2}} s. Find final displacement components/magnitude and average velocity components/magnitude.

First-leg components are (d1 cos theta,d1 sin theta); add (0,d2). Divide final displacement components by total time for average velocity.
