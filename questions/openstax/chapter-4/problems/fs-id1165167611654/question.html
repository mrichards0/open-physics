A dart is thrown horizontally at {{params.v}} m/s toward a target {{params.d}} m away. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find vertical drop and interpret how a skilled player compensates.

Aim above the desired hit point (or impart an upward component) so gravity's drop brings the dart to the target.Aim below the target because gravity bends the path upward.No compensation is needed because horizontal motion cancels gravity.

Flight time is d/v and drop=(1/2)g(d/v)^2. Players compensate for this predictable downward displacement.
