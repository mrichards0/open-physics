A rover leaves a level surface at {{params.v}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees on a world with g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find airborne time, range, and maximum height.

For same-height motion, T=2v0sin(theta)/g, R=v0cos(theta)T, and h=v0^2sin^2(theta)/(2g).
