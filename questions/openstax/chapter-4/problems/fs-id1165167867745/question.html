An airplane moving horizontally at {{params.kmh}} km/h drops a crate from {{params.h}} m. Ignore air resistance and use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find fall time and horizontal distance from release.

Fall time is sqrt(2h/g). Convert airplane speed to m/s and multiply by fall time.
