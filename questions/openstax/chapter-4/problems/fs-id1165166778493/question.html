A ball is launched at {{params.speed}} m/s and {{params.angle}} degrees on level ground where gravitational acceleration is g. What launch speed at the same angle produces the same range on a world where gravity is {{params.factor}}g?

The level-ground range is R=v0^2 sin(2 theta)/g. Holding R and theta fixed means v0^2/g is constant, so the speed scales as the square root of gravity.
