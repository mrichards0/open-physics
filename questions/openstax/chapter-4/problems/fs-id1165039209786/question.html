Rain falls vertically downward at {{params.rain}} m/s relative to Earth. A car travels horizontally east at {{params.car}} m/s. Use +x east and +y upward. Find the rain velocity measured in the car, its speed, and the angle below the backward horizontal.

v_rain/car=v_rain/Earth-v_car/Earth=(-v_car,-v_rain). Its magnitude follows from the Pythagorean theorem, and the requested acute angle is atan(v_rain/v_car).
