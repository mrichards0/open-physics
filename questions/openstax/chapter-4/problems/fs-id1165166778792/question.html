A level-ground jump covers {{params.range}} m at launch angle {{params.angle}} degrees. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find launch speed and total air time.

From R=v0^2 sin(2theta)/g, solve v0. Flight time is 2v0 sin(theta)/g.
