A jet rises vertically {{params.h}} m, then travels {{params.km}} km at {{params.angle}} degrees above horizontal. Find final horizontal and vertical displacement, magnitude, and angle above horizontal. Use meters.

Convert path length to meters. Add its components (L cos theta,L sin theta) to the initial vertical rise (0,h), then use hypot and atan2.
