A golfer's first displacement is ({{params.x1}},{{params.y1}}) m and second is ({{params.x2}},{{params.y2}}) m. Find final displacement components, magnitude, and standard direction counterclockwise from +x.

Add components. Magnitude is hypot(x,y), and atan2(y,x), normalized to 0–360 degrees, gives direction.
