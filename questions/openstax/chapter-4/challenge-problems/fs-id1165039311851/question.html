A truck is {{params.distance}} km west of an intersection, traveling east at {{params.truck}} km/h. At the intersection, a car travels north at {{params.car}} km/h. Find when the vehicles are closest and their minimum separation.

At time t their relative position is (-d+v_t t,-v_c t). Minimize its squared magnitude: differentiating gives t=dv_t/(v_t^2+v_c^2). Substitute this time into the separation formula.
