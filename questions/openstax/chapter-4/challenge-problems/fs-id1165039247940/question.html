A golf ball is launched {{params.angle}} degrees above horizontal toward a green {{params.distance}} m away and {{params.drop}} m below the tee. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find the required initial speed and time to reach the green.

Set y=-h in y=x tan(theta)-gx^2/[2v0^2cos^2(theta)] and solve for v0. Then use horizontal motion t=x/[v0cos(theta)].
