Which reference frames are commonly used instinctively while driving a car and flying in a commercial jet?

Both the vehicle/cabin frame and the road or Earth frame, switching according to what is being judged.Only a frame fixed to distant stars.Only the vehicle frame; the ground is irrelevant.No frame of reference is needed.

Passengers judge nearby objects in the vehicle frame while navigation and motion relative to destinations use an Earth-fixed frame.
