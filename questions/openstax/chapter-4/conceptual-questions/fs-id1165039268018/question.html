A clod falls from a constant-velocity truck and lands directly below the truck’s rear. Compare its velocity just before impact in truck and ground frames.

Relative to the truck:

Vertically downward.Forward and downward.Horizontally backward.

Relative to the ground:

Forward and downward, so the direction differs from the truck-frame velocity.Vertically downward, identical to the truck frame.Horizontally forward with no downward component.

The clod keeps the truck’s horizontal ground velocity. Subtracting truck velocity leaves only its downward component in the truck frame.
