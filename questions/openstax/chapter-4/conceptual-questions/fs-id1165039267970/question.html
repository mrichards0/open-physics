A passenger in a forward-moving pickup throws a ball straight backward. When can a roadside observer see it fall vertically, and what does the thrower see?

When backward throw speed relative to the truck equals truck speed; the roadside horizontal components cancel, while the thrower sees a backward projectile path.Whenever the ball is thrown backward at any speed; both observers see vertical fall.Only when the truck is stopped; the thrower sees the ball move forward.Never; velocities cannot cancel across frames.

Ground velocity is the vector sum of truck velocity and throw velocity. Equal opposite horizontal components cancel only in the ground frame.
