A hat falls from a jogger moving at constant velocity. Neglect air resistance. How do the paths compare?

The jogger sees a vertical fall; a stationary observer sees a forward-moving parabola.Both observers see a vertical fall.The jogger sees a parabola; the stationary observer sees a vertical fall.Both observers see the hat move backward in a straight line.

The hat retains the jogger’s horizontal ground velocity. Subtracting that common velocity in the jogger frame removes horizontal motion.
