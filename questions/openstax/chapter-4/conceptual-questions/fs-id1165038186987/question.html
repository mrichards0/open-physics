If instantaneous velocity is the zero vector, what is true of the position function at that instant?

Its derivative (tangent/slope vector) is zero.Its position must be the origin.Its acceleration must also be zero.Its path length must be zero for all time.

Instantaneous velocity is dr/dt, so zero velocity means every position-component derivative is zero at that instant.
