Why can a fast-moving basketball player dribble while looking mostly at other players?

The ball shares the player’s horizontal motion, so relative to the player its bounce is largely vertical and predictable.The ball has no velocity relative to the court.Gravity stops acting while the player runs.The ball follows the player because it is weightless.

Adding the player’s horizontal velocity to both player and ball leaves a simple, repeatable relative trajectory.
