For a projectile launched and landing at the same height, with neither horizontal nor vertical launch:

(a) Is velocity ever zero?

No; its horizontal component remains nonzero.Yes, at the apex.Yes, just before landing.

(b) When is speed minimum and maximum?

Minimum at the apex; equal maxima at launch and landing.Maximum at the apex; minimum at landing.Speed is constant throughout.

(c) Does the full velocity vector repeat after t=0?

No; at landing the vertical component has the opposite sign.Yes, at the apex.Yes, at landing.

(d) Does the initial speed repeat?

Yes, at the equal-height landing point.No, never.Only at the apex.

Horizontal velocity is constant; vertical velocity decreases through zero and reverses. Equal-height energy symmetry restores speed magnitude but not the original vector.
