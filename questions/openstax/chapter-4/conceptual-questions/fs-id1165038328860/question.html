An object has constant v_x and then experiences acceleration only in the y direction. Does v_x change?

No. With a_x=0, the x-component of velocity remains constant.Yes. Any acceleration changes every velocity component.Yes. v_x immediately becomes zero.No, because the entire velocity vector remains constant.

Vector components respond to their corresponding acceleration components independently; v_y changes but v_x does not.
