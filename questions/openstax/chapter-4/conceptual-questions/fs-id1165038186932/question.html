If distance traveled between every pair of points A and B equals |displacement from A to B|, what form must the trajectory have?

A straight line followed without reversing direction.Any circular arc.A zigzag path with constant speed.Any closed curve.

Path length equals endpoint separation only when no detour or reversal adds extra distance.
