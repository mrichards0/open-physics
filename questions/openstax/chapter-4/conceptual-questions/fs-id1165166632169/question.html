A sliding coin knocks another coin horizontally off a level table. Ignoring air resistance, which coin hits the floor first?

They hit simultaneously if they leave the same height at the same time.The faster horizontal coin hits first.The heavier coin hits first.The coin with no horizontal speed hits first.

Vertical fall time depends only on height and initial vertical velocity, not horizontal motion or mass.
