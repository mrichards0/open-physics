A rider launches at {{params.angle}} degrees, landing {{params.distance}} m horizontally away and {{params.drop}} m below the launch point. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find the required initial speed and flight time.

Use -h=x tan(theta)-gx^2/[2v0^2cos^2(theta)] and solve for v0. Then t=x/[v0cos(theta)].
