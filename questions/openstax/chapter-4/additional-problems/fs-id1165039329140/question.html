A rigid rod rotates at {{params.rate}} rev/s about one end. Compare points at radii {{params.r1}}, {{params.r2}}, and {{params.r3}} m by finding each centripetal acceleration and the ratios a2/a1 and a3/a1.

All points share angular speed omega=2 pi f. Since a_c=omega^2r, centripetal acceleration is directly proportional to radius.
