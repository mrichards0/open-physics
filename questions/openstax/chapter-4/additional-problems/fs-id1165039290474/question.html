A spacecraft initially has velocity ({{params.vx}}, {{params.vy}}, {{params.vz}}) m/s. Its rockets provide constant acceleration ({{params.ax}}, {{params.ay}}, {{params.az}}) m/s^2 for {{params.time}} s. Find the final velocity components and speed.

For constant acceleration, v_f=v_0+a Delta t component by component. The speed is the magnitude of the final velocity vector.
