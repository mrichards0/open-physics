A race car enters a curve of radius {{params.radius}} m at {{params.speed}} m/s. Find the centripetal acceleration required to follow the curve and its value as a multiple of g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

For instantaneous speed v on a path of radius r, a_c=v^2/r toward the center. Divide by g for the dimensionless comparison.
