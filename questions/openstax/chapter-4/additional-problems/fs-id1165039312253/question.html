An arrow is fired horizontally at a target {{params.distance}} m away and strikes {{params.drop_cm}} cm below the aiming point. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find its flight time and initial horizontal speed.

Vertical motion gives drop=(1/2)gt^2, so t=sqrt(2 drop/g), with drop converted to meters. Horizontal speed is distance divided by that time.
