Two boats have the same speed relative to a river and travel in opposite directions. A shore observer measures downstream speed {{params.downstream}} m/s and upstream speed {{params.upstream}} m/s. Find the boats' still-water speed and the river-current speed.

Downstream, v_d=v_b+u; upstream speed magnitude is v_u=v_b-u. Adding and subtracting give v_b=(v_d+v_u)/2 and u=(v_d-v_u)/2.
