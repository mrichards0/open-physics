A satellite follows a circular orbit of radius {{params.radius}} km with period {{params.period}} h. Find its orbital speed and centripetal acceleration.

Convert radius to meters and period to seconds. Speed is 2 pi r/T, and centripetal acceleration is v^2/r=4 pi^2r/T^2.
