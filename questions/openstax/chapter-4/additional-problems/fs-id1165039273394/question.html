A particle moves on a circle of radius {{params.radius}} m. At one instant its speed is {{params.speed}} m/s and its speed is increasing at {{params.tangent}} m/s^2. Find its radial and tangential acceleration components and total acceleration magnitude.

The perpendicular components are a_r=v^2/r and a_t=dv/dt. Their vector magnitude is sqrt(a_r^2+a_t^2).
