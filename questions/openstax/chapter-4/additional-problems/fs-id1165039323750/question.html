A braking car is on a circular curve of radius {{params.radius}} m. At the instant its speed is {{params.speed}} km/h, its speed is decreasing by {{params.rate}} km/h each second. Find the centripetal, signed tangential, and total acceleration. Take positive tangential direction along the motion.

Convert km/h to m/s by dividing by 3.6. Then a_r=v^2/r and a_t=-(rate/3.6); the components are perpendicular, so combine them with the Pythagorean theorem.
