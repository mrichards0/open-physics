A jumper achieves range {{params.best}} m at 45 degrees on level ground. Assuming the same launch speed, find the range at {{params.angle}} degrees and the distance lost relative to the 45-degree jump.

R=v0^2 sin(2 theta)/g. At 45 degrees sin(90 degrees)=1, so the new range is R_45 sin(2 theta), and loss is R_45-R_new.
