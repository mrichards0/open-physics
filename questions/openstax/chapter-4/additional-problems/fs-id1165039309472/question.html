At t=0 a particle is on the +x-axis, moving counterclockwise in uniform circular motion of radius {{params.radius}} m with centripetal acceleration {{params.accel}} m/s^2. Find its speed and angular speed, then its velocity components at t={{params.time}} s.

From a_c=v^2/R, v=sqrt(a_cR) and omega=v/R=sqrt(a_c/R). With initial phase zero, v(t)=(-v sin omega t,v cos omega t).
