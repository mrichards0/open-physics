A particle moves on a circle of radius {{params.radius}} km with speed v(t)=c1+c2 t^2, where c1={{params.c1}} m/s and c2={{params.c2}} m/s^3. At t={{params.time}} s, find its tangential, centripetal, and total acceleration. Also find when this nonrelativistic expression first predicts the speed of light c={{params.light}} m/s.

Differentiate v to get a_t=2c2t. The normal component is a_r=v^2/r, perpendicular to a_t, so a=sqrt(a_t^2+a_r^2). Solve c=c1+c2t^2 for the positive time.
