A particle moves counterclockwise in a circle of radius {{params.radius}} m centered at the origin with angular frequency {{params.omega}} rad/s. At t=0 it is at (R,0). At t={{params.time}} s, find its position, velocity, and acceleration components.

Use r=(R cos omega t,R sin omega t). Differentiation gives v=(-R omega sin omega t,R omega cos omega t) and a=(-R omega^2 cos omega t,-R omega^2 sin omega t).
