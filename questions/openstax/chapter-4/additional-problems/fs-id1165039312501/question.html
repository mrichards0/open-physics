On a planet, a projectile launched at {{params.speed}} m/s has maximum level-ground range {{params.range}} m. Find the planet's gravitational acceleration and the launch angle that produces this maximum range.

The maximum of R=v0^2 sin(2 theta)/g occurs when sin(2 theta)=1, at theta=45 degrees. Therefore g=v0^2/R.
