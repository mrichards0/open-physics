A propeller starts from rest. Its tip, radius {{params.radius}} m, has constant tangential acceleration {{params.tangent}} m/s^2 for {{params.time}} s. Find the tip speed, radial acceleration, and total acceleration at that time.

Starting from rest, v=a_tt. The radial component is v^2/r and is perpendicular to the constant tangential component. Thus total acceleration is their vector magnitude.
