A race car slows uniformly from {{params.initial}} m/s to {{params.final}} m/s in {{params.time}} s while following a curve of radius {{params.radius}} m. Find the signed tangential acceleration and the total acceleration magnitudes at the start and end.

The tangential acceleration is (v_f-v_i)/Delta t. At each endpoint a_r=v^2/r; combine that radial component with the same tangential component using sqrt(a_r^2+a_t^2).
