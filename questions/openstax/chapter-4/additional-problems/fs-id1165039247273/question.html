A point on a rotating spherical world is at latitude {{params.latitude}} degrees. The world has radius {{params.radius}} km and rotation period {{params.period}} h. Find the point's distance from the rotation axis, centripetal acceleration from rotation, and its ratio to g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

At latitude lambda the circular-path radius is R cos(lambda). With omega=2 pi/T, a_c=omega^2R cos(lambda). Convert R to meters and T to seconds.
