A particle has position r(t)=({{params.A}}t)i-({{params.B}}t^2)j, with SI units. Find the coefficients in v(t)=v_x i+v_y j and a(t)=a_x i+a_y j, then the initial velocity components.

Enter v_x=c1, v_y=c2 t, a_x=c3, and a_y=c4.

Differentiate position once: v=(A,-2Bt). Differentiate again: a=(0,-2B). At t=0 the initial velocity is (A,0).
