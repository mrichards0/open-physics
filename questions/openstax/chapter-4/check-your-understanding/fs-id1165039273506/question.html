A boat heads north at {{params.north}} m/s relative to water while the river flows east at {{params.east}} m/s. Find its Earth-frame east/north components, speed, and direction east of north.

Relative velocities add componentwise: v_Earth=(v_river east, v_boat north). Magnitude uses the Pythagorean theorem and angle east of north is atan2(v_east,v_north).
