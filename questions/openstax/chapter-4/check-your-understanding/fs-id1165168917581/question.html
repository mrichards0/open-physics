A point on a flywheel edge has radius {{params.r}} cm and centripetal acceleration {{params.ac}} cm/s^2. Find its speed in cm/s and m/s.

From a_c=v^2/r, v=sqrt(a_c r). Divide cm/s by 100 for m/s.
