Acceleration is the constant vector a=a_x i+a_y j+a_z k. What functional form must velocity have?

Each component is linear in time: v(t)=v0+(a_x i+a_y j+a_z k)t.Each velocity component is constant regardless of acceleration.Each component is quadratic in time.Only the speed can be found; component functions are undefined.

Velocity is the time integral of acceleration. Integrating each constant component gives a linear term plus its initial-velocity constant.
