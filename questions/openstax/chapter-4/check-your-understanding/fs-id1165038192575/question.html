A particle has r(t)=({{params.c}}t^3)i+({{params.y}})j meters. Find instantaneous velocity components at t={{params.tm}} s and average velocity components from t={{params.t1}} to {{params.t2}} s. Are the two velocity vectors equal?

No; for cubic position, the secant slope over the symmetric interval differs from the tangent slope at its midpoint.Yes; average and instantaneous velocity are always equal at the midpoint.Yes; the constant y-coordinate makes every velocity zero.

Differentiate x=ct^3 to get v_x=3ct^2 and v_y=0. Average v_x=c(t2^3-t1^3)/(t2-t1); average v_y=0.
