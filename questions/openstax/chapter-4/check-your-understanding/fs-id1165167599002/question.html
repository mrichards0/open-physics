A rock is launched horizontally at {{params.v0}} m/s from a cliff {{params.h}} m high. Choose the release point as origin, +x along launch, and +y upward; use g={{params.g}} m/s^2.

Which component equations apply?

x=v0t and y=-(1/2)gt^2.x=(1/2)gt^2 and y=v0t.x=v0t-(1/2)gt^2 and y=0.

Horizontal acceleration is zero; vertical acceleration is -g. Set y=-h to find t=sqrt(2h/g). Then v_x=v0, v_y=-gt, and speed is the component magnitude.
