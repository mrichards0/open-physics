Two golf balls launch from level ground at the same {{params.v}} m/s. Shot 1 uses {{params.a1}} degrees and shot 2 uses {{params.a2}} degrees. Use g={{params.g}} m/s^2. Find both ranges and identify the greater.

Shot 1 has greater range because its angle is closer to 45 degrees.Shot 2 has greater range because lower angles always travel farther.The ranges must be equal for any two launch angles.

For equal launch and landing heights, R=v^2 sin(2theta)/g. Range is largest at 45 degrees; generated shot 1 is closer to that optimum than shot 2.
