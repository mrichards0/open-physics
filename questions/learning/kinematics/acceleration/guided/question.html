Step 3 — Calculate acceleration

For any change, subtract the initial value from the final value. Use $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$, followed by $a=\Delta v/\Delta t$. For this variant, $\Delta v={{params.vf}}-{{params.vi}}$ m/s and $\Delta t={{params.dt}}$ s. $\Delta v={{params.delta_v}}$ m/s, so $a={{params.delta_v}}/{{params.dt}}={{params.acceleration}}$ m/s². Enter these values to complete the guided stage.

Average acceleration is the velocity change divided by elapsed time:

$\Delta v={{params.vf}}-{{params.vi}}={{params.delta_v}}\ \mathrm{m/s}$ and $a={{params.delta_v}}/{{params.dt}}={{params.acceleration}}\ \mathrm{m/s^2}$.
