Which physical quantity describes the rate at which velocity changes?

Displacement Average velocity Average acceleration Elapsed time Acceleration describes how quickly velocity changes. For any change, subtract the initial value from the final value: $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$. For this variant, $\Delta v={{params.vf}}-{{params.vi}}$ m/s. Average acceleration is velocity change divided by elapsed time: $a=\Delta v/\Delta t$. $a={{params.delta_v}}/{{params.dt}}={{params.acceleration}}$ m/s².
