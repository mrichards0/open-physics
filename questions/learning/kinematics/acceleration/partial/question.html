A vehicle's velocity changes uniformly from {{params.vi}} m/s to {{params.vf}} m/s in {{params.dt}} s.

Use the two requested quantities to organize your solution.

 First calculate the signed change in velocity, then divide it by elapsed time. $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$ and $a=\Delta v/\Delta t$. Use $\Delta v={{params.vf}}-{{params.vi}}$ m/s and divide the result by {{params.dt}} s.

$\Delta v={{params.delta_v}}$ m/s and $a={{params.acceleration}}$ m/s².
