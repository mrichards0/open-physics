Learning 1 — Make the units cancel

A conversion factor equals 1 because its numerator and denominator describe the same length.

Example from the method in OpenStax §1.3:
$1250\,\cancel{\mathrm{m}}\times\dfrac{1\,\mathrm{km}}{1000\,\cancel{\mathrm{m}}}=1.25\,\mathrm{km}$

Build the unit-only factor that converts meters to kilometers. Put the desired unit on top and the unit being canceled on the bottom.

$\mathrm{m}\times$

$=\mathrm{km}$

Meters must be in the denominator so they cancel the meters you started with. Place kilometers in the numerator and meters in the denominator.

$\mathrm{m}\times\dfrac{\mathrm{km}}{\mathrm{m}}=\mathrm{km}$. The numerical equality is supplied in Learning 2.
