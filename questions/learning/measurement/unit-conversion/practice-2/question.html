Practice 2 — Convert a cubed unit

A cube has side length {{params.side}} m. Express its volume in cm³.

 One meter equals 100 centimeters. For volume, cube the entire length conversion: $(100\,\mathrm{cm}/1\,\mathrm{m})^3$.

$V=({{params.side}}\,\mathrm{m})^3\left(\dfrac{100\,\mathrm{cm}}{1\,\mathrm{m}}\right)^3={{correct_answers.volume_cm3}}\,\mathrm{cm^3}$.
