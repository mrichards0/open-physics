Practice 1 — Chain two conversion factors

A vehicle travels at {{params.speed}} km/h. Convert its speed to m/s.

 Use $1\,\mathrm{h}=3600\,\mathrm{s}$. Use $1\,\mathrm{km}=1000\,\mathrm{m}$. Arrange the factors so both km and h cancel: multiply by $1000\,\mathrm{m}/1\,\mathrm{km}$ and $1\,\mathrm{h}/3600\,\mathrm{s}$.

${{params.speed}}\,\dfrac{\cancel{\mathrm{km}}}{\cancel{\mathrm{h}}}\times\dfrac{1000\,\mathrm{m}}{1\,\cancel{\mathrm{km}}}\times\dfrac{1\,\cancel{\mathrm{h}}}{3600\,\mathrm{s}}={{correct_answers.meters_per_second}}\,\mathrm{m/s}$.
