An object moves along a straight horizontal axis. Its initial position is $x_0 = {{params.x0}}\ \mathrm{m}$ and its final position is $x_f = {{params.xf}}\ \mathrm{m}$.

Calculate the object's displacement $\Delta x$.

Displacement is final position minus initial position: $\Delta x = x_f-x_0 = {{params.xf}}\ \mathrm{m} - ({{params.x0}}\ \mathrm{m}) = {{correct_answers.displacement}}\ \mathrm{m}$.
