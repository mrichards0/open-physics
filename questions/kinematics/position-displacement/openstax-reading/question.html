Position and displacement

Position describes where an object is relative to a chosen frame of reference. Displacement is the signed change from its initial position to its final position:

$\Delta x = x_f - x_0$

Read OpenStax §3.1: Position, Displacement, and Average Velocity before answering.

Positive, because the final position is greater than the initial position. Negative, because the professor began away from the origin. Zero, because both positions are measured from the same origin.
